* Problem
:PROPERTIES:
:FAMILY: exp
:CLASS: exponential-cone (channel-capacity / water-filling)
:SOURCE: Boyd & Vandenberghe, "Convex Optimization", Sec 4.6.2 / 5.5.4 (water-filling); closed form derived independently below
:KNOWN_OPTIMAL: 3.1243616245
:N_VARIABLES: 6 (3 power vars P_i, 3 epigraph vars t_i)
:N_CONSTRAINTS: 1 nonneg (budget) + 3 nonneg (P_i>=0) + 3 exp cones
:SELECTED_BY: log-rotation
:COVERAGE_TARGET: -
:END:

\begin{align}
\max\quad & \sum_i \log(1 + P_i/N_i) \\
\text{s.t.}\quad & \sum_i P_i \le P_{\text{total}},\ P_i \ge 0
\end{align}

with \(N=(1,2,4)\), \(P_{\text{total}}=10\).

Water-filling KKT solution: \(P_i^* = \max(0, \mu - N_i)\), \(\mu\) chosen so
\(\sum P_i^* = P_{\text{total}}\). With all channels active,
\(\mu = (P_{\text{total}}+\sum N_i)/n = 17/3 \approx 5.6667 > \max(N)=4\)
(confirms all-active assumption), giving \(P^*=(4.6667,3.6667,1.6667)\) and
\(f^* = n\log\mu - \log\prod N_i = 3.1243616245\).

* Cross-check / Verification
Objective agrees with the closed-form water-filling optimum and with both
cvxpy backends to ~1e-8 at default tolerance already. The solution vector
`P`, however, showed a larger discrepancy at pounce's default tol
(x_inf_err = 2.17e-4, and status flagged `optimal_inaccurate`). This is
consistent with an ill-conditioned mapping from residual complementarity
gap to x-space here — CLARABEL's own default-tol solution is off from the
closed form by a comparable ~1.6e-4 in x, even though its objective is
essentially exact. Tightening pounce's `tol` to 1e-9 (`max_iter=200`)
resolved both symptoms: status flips to `optimal`, x_inf_err drops to
2.71e-5. This is ordinary interior-point tolerance behavior (flat objective
near the optimum => amplified x-sensitivity), not a solver defect — the
same shape of finding as the previously-fixed #336 (loose default tol
flagging a correct point as inaccurate), but this instance resolves via the
standard user-facing `tol` knob rather than indicating a stale threshold
bug, so no new issue is warranted.

* Verdict
PASS — pounce's exp-cone water-filling solve matches the closed-form KKT
optimum and both cvxpy backends to ~1e-8 in objective, and to ~3e-5 in x
once `tol` is tightened past the default. Not filed: this is expected
interior-point behavior on an objective with a flat direction near the
optimum, and default-tol `optimal_inaccurate` is an honest self-report, not
a wrong answer.
