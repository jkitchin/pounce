:N_VARS: 3  (R, x1, x2)
:N_CONSTRAINTS: 12 rows = 4 SOC cones, each dim 3
:CONE_LAYOUT: cones=[("soc",3)]*4; per point p_i, s=(R, x1-p_ix, x2-p_iy) in K_soc^3, s=h-Gv with rows [-1,0,0]/h=0, [0,-1,0]/h=-p_ix, [0,0,-1]/h=-p_iy
:KNOWN_OPTIMAL: 0.7071067811865476  (= 1/sqrt(2)), center (0.5, 0.5)
:END:

** Problem
Minimum enclosing ball of the 4 corners of the unit square
p = (0,0),(1,0),(0,1),(1,1):
   minimize   R
   subject to ||x_c - p_i||_2 <= R,  i = 1..4
over (R, x_c). By symmetry the center is the centroid (0.5, 0.5) and the
radius equals half the square's diagonal = sqrt(0.5^2 + 0.5^2) = 1/sqrt(2).

** Cone encoding (the trap)
pounce: s = h - G x in the listed cones; soc dim d => s0 >= ||s_{1:}||_2.
Each point contributes one dim-3 soc cone:
  s0 = R            -> G row [-1, 0, 0],  h  0
  s1 = x1 - p_ix    -> G row [ 0,-1, 0],  h -p_ix
  s2 = x2 - p_iy    -> G row [ 0, 0,-1],  h -p_iy
=> R >= ||x_c - p_i||_2. Verified per-row sign and dim before running.
