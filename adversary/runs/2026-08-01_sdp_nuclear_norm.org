:PROPERTIES:

:SOURCE: Fazel, Hindi, Boyd, "A Rank Minimization Heuristic..." (ACC 2001);

$\|A\|_* = \min_{W_1,W_2} \tfrac12(\mathrm{tr}(W_1)+\mathrm{tr}(W_2))$ s.t.
$\begin{bmatrix}W_1 & A\\A^\top & W_2\end{bmatrix} \succeq 0$, for a fixed
$3\times4$ dense matrix $A$.
