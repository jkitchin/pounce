#+TITLE: Adversary Run: Hard-Margin SVM via SOC Epigraph
#+DATE: 2026-08-05

* Problem
:PROPERTIES:
:FAMILY: socp
:CLASS: SOC epigraph + linear (nonneg) constraints, symmetric closed-form
:SOURCE: Boyd & Vandenberghe, Convex Optimization, Sec 8.6.1 "Linear discrimination" / classic maximum-margin SVM (Vapnik)
:KNOWN_OPTIMAL: 1.0
:N_VARIABLES: 4 (t, w1, w2, b)
:N_CONSTRAINTS: cones=[("soc",3), ("nonneg",4)]
:SELECTED_BY: log-rotation
:COVERAGE_TARGET: -
:END:

4 points in \(\mathbb{R}^2\), symmetric by construction so the optimum is
known exactly by inspection: class +1 at \((1,1),(1,-1)\), class \(-1\) at
\((-1,1),(-1,-1)\).

\begin{align}
\min\quad & t \\
\text{s.t.}\quad & \|w\|_2 \le t \\
& y_i(w^\top x_i + b) \ge 1,\ i=1..4
\end{align}

By symmetry the maximum-margin hyperplane is \(x=0\) (\(w^*=(1,0)\),
\(b^*=0\)), with all 4 points on their margin (\(y_i(w^{*\top}x_i+b^*)=1\)
for every \(i\), verified by direct substitution before use). Known
optimal \(t^* = \|w^*\|_2 = 1\).

This exercises pounce's actual conic entry point (`solve_socp`) rather
than the more common \(\min \tfrac12\|w\|^2\) QP form of hard-margin SVM,
which would route through the QP solver instead.

* Cross-check / Verification
- cvxpy CLARABEL and SCS (independent conic solvers, same SOCP model):
  both agree with pounce to <3e-10, and agree with each other to 1.02e-10.
- All four margin constraints evaluated at pounce's own solution:
  \(y_i(w^\top x_i+b) = [1,1,1,1]\) exactly (to display precision) — every
  point active, matching the closed-form prediction that all 4 points are
  support vectors.
- Cone dimensions re-derived from scratch before formulating (SOC block
  dim 3 for \((t,w_1,w_2)\), nonneg block dim 4 for the margin rows) rather
  than trusted by assumption.

* Verdict
PASS — pounce's `solve_socp` correctly solves a hard-margin SVM posed via
genuine SOC epigraph (not the more commonly tested QP form), matching the
closed-form symmetric solution and two independent cvxpy backends to
~1e-10. No defect found.
