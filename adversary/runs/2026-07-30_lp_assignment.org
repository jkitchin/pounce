minimize \sum_{i,j} c_{ij} x_{ij} s.t. \sum_j x_{ij}=1 \forall i, \sum_i x_{ij}=1 \forall j, x_{ij}\ge0

The assignment-problem constraint matrix is totally unimodular, so the LP
relaxation's optimal vertex is automatically integral (Birkhoff-von Neumann);
no explicit x_{ij}<=1 upper bound was even imposed, testing that pounce's LP
path reaches the integral vertex from TU + nonnegativity alone.
