\[
\min -3x_1 - 9x_2 \quad \text{s.t.} \quad x_1+4x_2 \le 8,\ x_1+2x_2 \le 4,\ x\ge 0
\]

Known optimal vertex \((0,2)\), \(Z=18\). In standard form with slacks
\(s_1=8-x_1-4x_2\), \(s_2=4-x_1-2x_2\), both \(s_1=s_2=0\) at the optimum
while \(x_1=0\) too -- so of the two basic variables required at a BFS
in this 2-constraint LP, only \(x_2\) is actually nonzero: a textbook
degenerate optimal basis, which stresses the simplex/crossover ratio-test
tie-break rather than a generic interior vertex.
