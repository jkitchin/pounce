* Problem
:PROPERTIES:
:FAMILY: exp
:CLASS: entropy maximization (relative-entropy epigraph via Kexp, equality constraint)
:SOURCE: Cover & Thomas, "Elements of Information Theory", Thm 2.6.4; Boyd & Vandenberghe, "Convex Optimization" Sec 5.2 style. Uniform distribution maximizes entropy on the simplex.
:KNOWN_OPTIMAL: -log(4) = -1.3862943611198906
:N_VARIABLES: 8 (4 x_i, 4 t_i)
:N_CONSTRAINTS: 1 equality row; 4 Kexp cones of dim 3
:SELECTED_BY: log-rotation
:COVERAGE_TARGET: -
:END:

minimize $\sum_i x_i \log x_i$ s.t. $\sum_i x_i = 1$, $x \ge 0$, via the
non-symmetric exponential-cone triple $(t_i, x_i, 1) \in K_{\exp}$
(pounce convention $\{(x,y,z): y e^{x/y} \le z\}$), enforcing
$t_i \le \mathrm{entr}(x_i)$, and minimizing $-\sum_i t_i$.

oracle and the textbook closed form. This exercises the (t_i, x_i, 1) Kexp

probe, plus an equality (not just inequality) constraint.
