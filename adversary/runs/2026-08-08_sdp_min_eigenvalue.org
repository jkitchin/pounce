* Problem
:PROPERTIES:
:FAMILY: sdp
:CLASS: 1-variable PSD-cone program on a non-diagonal 3x3 matrix (genuine svec off-diagonal sqrt(2) scaling)
:SOURCE: Boyd & Vandenberghe, Convex Optimization, Sec 4.6.2 ("Minimum eigenvalue via SDP"): lambda_min(A) = max{t : A - tI >= 0}
:KNOWN_OPTIMAL: 1.0 (lambda_min of A = [[2,1,0],[1,3,1],[0,1,2]], via numpy/LAPACK eigvalsh)
:N_VARIABLES: 1
:N_CONSTRAINTS: PSD cone, n=3 (svec dim 6)
:SELECTED_BY: log-rotation
:COVERAGE_TARGET: - (sdp tied-least-tested at 11, last run 2026-08-06)
:END:

\[
\max_t\ t \quad\text{s.t.}\quad A - tI \succeq 0,\qquad A=\begin{pmatrix}2&1&0\\1&3&1\\0&1&2\end{pmatrix}
\]

Cast as ~solve_socp~ with a single variable \(t\), \(G\) the single column
\(\mathrm{svec}(I)\), \(h=\mathrm{svec}(A)\), cone \(("psd",3)\), objective
\(\min -t\). \(A\) is symmetric tridiagonal (not diagonal), so the
off-diagonal \(\sqrt2\) svec scaling is genuinely exercised, unlike the
previously-logged MAX-CUT/C4 problem (2026-08-06), whose relaxation matrix
was diagonal-dominant but the probe there used a different (bipartite
graph Laplacian) structure.

Known optimal: 1.0

pounce's reported \(t^*\) matches the LAPACK direct eigensolver (a
completely non-SDP, non-cone independent computation) to \(3\times10^{-10}\)
relative error, and matches cvxpy's own independent SDP formulation to
\(2.8\times10^{-9}\). The PSD residual check -- \(\lambda_{\min}(A-t^*I)\)
recomputed via a fresh LAPACK eigendecomposition -- gives \(3.18\times
10^{-10}\), confirming the returned point is (numerically) feasible, not
just numerically close to the right scalar.

* Cross-check / Verification
Two independent oracles: (1) numpy/LAPACK ~eigvalsh~, a direct dense
eigensolver with no relation to any conic/SDP algorithm; (2) cvxpy/Clarabel,
an independently-implemented SDP solver. Both agree with pounce to
\(<3\times10^{-9}\).

* Verdict
PASS -- pounce's PSD cone (svec/smat round-trip, including the
off-diagonal \(\sqrt2\) scaling on a genuinely non-diagonal matrix) is
correct against both a direct linear-algebra oracle and an independent SDP
solver.
