* Problem
:PROPERTIES:
:FAMILY: lp
:CLASS: bounded LP, presolve-relevant degeneracy (fixed var + redundant row)
:SOURCE: self-constructed instance; optimum derived analytically by a
  dominance argument on the simplex slice {x1+x2+x3=10, 0<=xi<=10}
:KNOWN_OPTIMAL: 38.0
:N_VARIABLES: 4
:N_CONSTRAINTS: 2 equalities (one redundant) + box bounds
:SELECTED_BY: log-rotation
:COVERAGE_TARGET: - (coverage-combined.sh skipped per environment adaptations; log rotation used instead)
:END:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\min_{x} \quad & x_1 + 2x_2 + 3x_3 + 4x_4 \\
\text{s.t.} \quad & x_1+x_2+x_3 = 10 \\
& 2x_1+2x_2+2x_3 = 20 \quad (\text{redundant, } = 2\times\text{row 1}) \\
& x_4 = 7 \quad (\mathrm{lb}_4=\mathrm{ub}_4=7) \\
& 0 \le x_1,x_2,x_3 \le 10
\end{aligned}
\]

Since row 2 is an exact scalar multiple of row 1, the feasible set in
$(x_1,x_2,x_3)$ collapses to the simplex slice $x_1+x_2+x_3=10$. On that
slice, minimizing $x_1+2x_2+3x_3$ is a pure dominance argument: put all
mass on the cheapest-cost coordinate $x_1$ (coefficient 1), which is
reachable within its bound ($x_1 \le 10$ covers the whole required sum).
$x_4$ is pinned by its bounds regardless of its cost coefficient.
