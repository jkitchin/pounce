* Problem
:PROPERTIES:
:FAMILY: diff
:CLASS: SOCP layer gradient w.r.t. h (cone RHS) -- prior diff-family probes
  covered dP/dc for the QP layer and dG for the SOCP layer; dh for the
  SOCP layer was untested in the log.
:SOURCE: closed-form solution of "minimize c'x s.t. ||x-a||_2<=r" is the
  projection of the linear-cost direction onto the ball boundary,
  x*(a)=a-r*c/||c||_2, giving EXACT closed-form Jacobians dx*/da=I_n and
  dObj/da=c (both independent of a's value) -- not merely a
  finite-difference oracle.
:KNOWN_OPTIMAL: obj*(a0) = c.a0 - r*||c||_2 = -4.8063465777e+00 (seed 21, n=4, r=2.5)
:N_VARIABLES: 4
:N_CONSTRAINTS: 1 SOC block, dim 5
:SELECTED_BY: log-rotation
:COVERAGE_TARGET: - (log-rotation driven; coverage-combined.sh skipped per environment adaptations)
:END:

$a$ enters only through $h=(r,a)$, with $G$'s first row all-zero and rows
$1..n = I_n$, so $s=h-Gx=(r,a-x)$ isolates the SOCP layer's dependence on
$h$:
\[
\min_x\ c^\top x \quad\text{s.t.}\quad \|x-a\|_2\le r
\qquad\Rightarrow\qquad
x^*(a) = a - r\frac{c}{\|c\|_2},\qquad
\mathrm{obj}^*(a) = c^\top a - r\|c\|_2.
\]
\[
\frac{\partial x^*}{\partial a} = I_n \qquad
\frac{\partial\, \mathrm{obj}^*}{\partial a} = c \quad (\text{constant, independent of } a).
\]
