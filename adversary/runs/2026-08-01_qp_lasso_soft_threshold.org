* Problem
:PROPERTIES:
:FAMILY: qp
:CLASS: bound-constrained QP (L1-regularized least squares, variable-split)
:SOURCE: standard LASSO-as-QP reformulation (Boyd & Vandenberghe, "Convex
  Optimization", Sec 6.1 / EE364a notes); closed form for orthogonal design
  is the soft-threshold / L1-proximal operator (Beck, "First-Order Methods
  in Optimization", 2017, Sec 6.3-6.5).
:KNOWN_OPTIMAL: 7.145 (exact, rational arithmetic)
:N_VARIABLES: 10 (5 split into u,v >= 0)
:N_CONSTRAINTS: 0 equalities, 10 bound constraints
:SELECTED_BY: log-rotation
:COVERAGE_TARGET: -
:END:

minimize $0.5\|u-v-b\|_2^2 + \lambda \mathbf{1}^\top(u+v)$ s.t. $u,v \ge 0$,
with $A=I$ (orthogonal design), $b=(3.0,-0.5,0.2,-4.0,1.5)$, $\lambda=1$.
Closed form: $x_i^* = \mathrm{sign}(b_i)\max(|b_i|-\lambda,0)$.

(no slack in the reformulation's exactness assumption).

* Cross-check / Verification
Two independent checks: (1) exact-rational closed-form soft-threshold
operator (orthogonal design, $A=I$), (2) cvxpy/CLARABEL solving the same
LASSO problem via its native (non-split) $\ell_1$ atom — a structurally
different formulation of the same problem, ruling out a variable-splitting
artifact. All three agree to $<10^{-7}$.
