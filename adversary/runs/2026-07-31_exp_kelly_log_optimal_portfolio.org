#+TITLE: Adversary Run: Log-Optimal (Kelly) Portfolio via the Exponential Cone
#+DATE: 2026-07-31

* Problem
:PROPERTIES:
:FAMILY: exp
:CLASS: exp-cone (log-utility maximization over discrete scenarios)
:SOURCE: J.L. Kelly Jr., "A New Interpretation of Information Rate", Bell Sys. Tech. J. 35 (1956); Cover & Thomas, "Elements of Information Theory" 2nd ed., Ch 16; modeled per the CVXPY/MOSEK "log-optimal investment" exp-cone cookbook pattern
:KNOWN_OPTIMAL: none published for this instance (oracle-only; true optimum is the boundary point w=(1,0,0), growth=0)
:N_VARIABLES: 7 (3 weights + 4 scenario epigraph vars)
:N_CONSTRAINTS: 1 equality, nonneg cone (3) + 4x exp cone (3 each)
:SELECTED_BY: log-rotation
:COVERAGE_TARGET: -
:END:

\[
\max_w \; \tfrac{1}{K}\sum_{k=1}^K \log(r_k^\top w) \quad \text{s.t.} \quad \mathbf{1}^\top w = 1,\; w \ge 0
\]

Two independent bets (asset 0 = cash, gross return 1.0 always; asset 1
doubles/halves p=1/2; asset 2 gains 20%/loses 40% p=1/2, independent of
asset 1) give \(K=4\) equally-likely scenarios. Each scenario's log term is
encoded via pounce's exp-cone convention \((x,y,z)\in K_{\exp} \iff
y\,e^{x/y}\le z\): setting \(x=t_k, y=1, z=r_k^\top w\) enforces
\(t_k \le \log(r_k^\top w)\).

Both individual risky bets have negative expected log-return
(\(E[\log A]\approx-0.144\), \(E[\log B]\approx-0.164\)), so with a
riskless asset available the true Kelly-optimal allocation is all-cash,
growth = 0 — a boundary/degenerate solution, a good stress case for the
non-symmetric HSDE driver's boundary handling.

* Cross-check / Verification
Two independent oracles: cvxpy's native exp-cone support (CLARABEL) and a
completely cone-free smooth NLP (scipy SLSQP directly maximizing the sum of
logs). All three agree to ~1.3e-7 in growth rate and ~8e-4 in the weight
vector (the latter gap explained by all three IPM-style methods leaving
similar residual mass away from the exact vertex at their respective
default tolerances).

* Verdict
PASS — pounce's exp-cone driver matches both an independent conic solver
and an independent smooth-NLP solver to well within tolerance, including on
a boundary-degenerate instance. No defect found.
