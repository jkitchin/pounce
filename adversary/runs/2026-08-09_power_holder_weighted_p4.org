* Problem
:PROPERTIES:
:FAMILY: power
:CLASS: p-norm epigraph (p=4), non-uniform linear equality
:SOURCE: Boyd & Vandenberghe, "Convex Optimization" Sec 3.1.2 Hoelder-duality/Lagrange-stationarity closed form for min sum|x_i|^p s.t. a^Tx=b.
:KNOWN_OPTIMAL: computed exactly via c=b/sum(a_i^{4/3}); obj=c^4*sum(a_i^{4/3}) = 4.2922199444e+01 for a=(1,2,3), b=12
:N_VARIABLES: 6 (3 x_i, 3 t_i)
:N_CONSTRAINTS: 1 equality row; 3 pow cones (alpha=1/4)
:SELECTED_BY: log-rotation
:COVERAGE_TARGET: -
:END:

minimize $\sum_i x_i^4$ s.t. $a^Tx = b$, $a=(1,2,3)$, $b=12$, via pow-cone
triples $(x_i, t_i, 1)$ with $\alpha=1/4$ enforcing $x_i^4 \le t_i$.
Closed form (Lagrange stationarity $4x_i^3=\lambda a_i$): $x_i = c\,a_i^{1/3}$,
$c = b/\sum a_i^{4/3}$.
