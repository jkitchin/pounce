7 randomly generated small convex QPs (random-seeded, SPD Hessian via
\(A A^\top + \tfrac12 I\), feasible equality/inequality RHS constructed
from a known interior point) solved both via ~solve_qp_batch~ and via 7
individual ~solve_qp~ calls. Prior batch-family runs in the log checked
\(x^*\)/obj/status parity; this run additionally checks the *full KKT
multiplier vectors* — equality \(y\), inequality \(z\), and bound
\(z_{lb}/z_{ub}\) — which a batched interior-point implementation could
plausibly mis-index or fail to correctly unscale per-instance even while
getting \(x^*\) right.
