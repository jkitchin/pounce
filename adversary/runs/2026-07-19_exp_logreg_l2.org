#+TITLE: Adversary Run: L2-regularized logistic regression (exp cone)
#+DATE: <2026-07-19>

* Problem
:PROPERTIES:
:FAMILY: exp
:CLASS: logistic regression / softplus epigraph via exponential cone, closed-form optimum
:SOURCE: Boyd & Vandenberghe, /Convex Optimization/ (2004), sec. 7.1.1 (logistic regression MLE); MOSEK Modeling Cookbook v3.3 sec. 5.2.5 (log-sum-exp) and 5.3 (logistic regression) for the exp-cone epigraph. Instance constructed so the optimum is analytic.
:KNOWN_OPTIMAL: 1.9353361793e+00
:N_VARIABLES: 8 (w0, w1, ta, tb, z1..z4)
:N_CONSTRAINTS: 4 x ("exp",3) + ("nonneg",2) = 14 rows, no equalities
:END:

Minimize over $w \in \mathbb{R}^2$

\[
 f(w) = \sum_i \log\!\left(1 + e^{-y_i w^\top x_i}\right) + \tfrac{\lambda}{2}\|w\|_2^2 .
\]

The data are $m=2$ copies of $a$ and $k=3$ copies of $b$ (all $y_i=+1$), with
$a \perp b$ and both rotated by $R(30^\circ)$ so the solver sees a coupled 2-D
problem. Orthogonality separates the objective in $u_a=a^\top w$, $u_b=b^\top w$:

\[
 g(u) = n_g \log(1+e^{-u}) + \tfrac{\lambda}{2}\frac{u^2}{\|a\|^2},
 \qquad g'(u)=0 \iff \|a\|^2 = \frac{(1+e^{u^\star})\lambda u^\star}{n_g}.
\]

Choosing $\|a\|^2 = 2\log 3$, $\|b\|^2 = \tfrac{5}{3}\log 4$, $\lambda=1$ pins
$u_a^\star=\log 3$, $u_b^\star=\log 4$ exactly, giving

\[
 f^\star = 2\log\tfrac43 + 3\log\tfrac54
 + \tfrac12\left[\frac{(\log 3)^2}{2\log 3} + \frac{(\log 4)^2}{\tfrac53\log 4}\right]
 = 1.9353361793\ldots
\]

Exp-cone epigraph used: $\log(1+e^{-u}) \le t \iff e^{-u-t} + e^{-t} \le 1$,
i.e. $(-u-t,1,z_1)\in K_{\exp}$, $(-t,1,z_2)\in K_{\exp}$, $z_1+z_2\le 1$.
The quadratic regularizer is carried in ~P~ (quadratic objective on the
non-symmetric HSDE path).

* Cross-check / Verification
- Two independent cvxpy solvers (SCS and CLARABEL) agree with each other to
  1e-10 and with the analytic $f^\star$ to 1e-11; pounce agrees with both to
  1.3e-8.
- *Cone encoding verified read-only.* ~crates/pounce-convex/src/cones/exp.rs~
  defines $K_{\exp}=\mathrm{cl}\{(x,y,z): y e^{x/y}\le z,\ y>0\}$ (Clarabel/MOSEK
  orientation), and ~solve_socp~ requires the slack $s = h - Gx$ to lie in each
  cone block. cvxpy's ~ExpCone(x,y,z)~ uses the *same* ordering, so no
  permutation is involved here (unlike the power cone). The rows were built as
  $s = (-u-t,\,1,\,z_1)$ etc. and checked against the doctest GP in
  ~python/pounce/qp.py~ (min $x + 1/x = 2$), which reproduces 2.0 with status
  ~optimal~.
- Alternative formulation cross-check: moving the quadratic regularizer out of
  ~P~ into an explicit rotated-SOC epigraph (mixing ~soc~ with ~exp~ on the
  non-symmetric driver) gives 1.935336105, rel err 3.8e-8 vs known -- the same
  answer, so the ~P~-on-nonsym path is not the source of the residual floor.
- Tolerance sweep: ~tol~ in {default, 1e-8, 1e-10, 1e-12} x ~max_iter~ in
  {200, 1000} all return ~optimal_inaccurate~; ~tol=1e-10~ improves the answer
  to rel err 1.45e-9. The status never becomes ~optimal~.

* Verdict
*PASS* (with a ~TOLERANCE~/status note). pounce's answer is correct: 1.27e-8
relative on the objective against an analytic optimum and two independent
cvxpy solvers, four orders of magnitude inside the 1e-4 protocol tolerance.

The only wrinkle is the reported status, ~optimal_inaccurate~ rather than
~optimal~. Reading ~crates/pounce-convex/src/hsde_nonsym.rs~ (lines 1218--1247)
this is *documented, deliberate behaviour*: the non-symmetric driver ended in
~NumericalFailure~/~IterationLimit~ (a near-boundary stall), and because the
best iterate's KKT residual (3.67e-8, complementarity-dominated) is below
$\sqrt{\mathrm{tol}} = 10^{-4}$ it is restored and labelled
~OptimalInaccurate~ -- explicitly "not a bare ~Optimal~" so callers can tell it
apart. The clean convergence test at ~tol~=1e-8 is missed by roughly 4x on the
complementarity residual. That is a mild conservatism in the stopping test, not
a wrong answer, and it is not filed as a bug. Callers must therefore accept
~optimal_inaccurate~ as a usable solve on the exp-cone path.
