|----------+-------------+-------+-------|
| C1 | $\min -\log x + x/M,\ x\ge1$ | $M$ | $1-\log M$ |

| C3 | $\min -x + x^2/(2M),\ x\ge0$ | $M$ | $-M/2$ |
| C4 | $\min -x + (x/M)^8,\ x\ge0$ | $M^{8/7}/8^{1/7}$ | $-x^*+(x^*/M)^8$ |

drop per doubling of $x$ grows **linearly** — the strongest possible
"non-decelerating descent along a growing ray" signal — yet the answer is the
finite $-M/2$.

** The finding

Two provably bounded instances are reported **UNBOUNDED** by the default
(auto) route. Minimal reproducer:

#+begin_src
min  -x + x^2/(2M)      s.t.  x >= 0,     M = 1e12
#+end_src

$P=1/M=10^{-12}>0$, strictly convex and coercive, unique global minimum
$x^*=10^{12}$, $f^*=-5\times10^{11}$. POUNCE:

#+begin_src
Problem class: convex QP. Selected solver: convex QP interior-point (pounce-convex) [solver_selection=auto].
POUNCE (convex QP IPM, pounce-convex): Problem is unbounded (dual infeasible).  obj=-399.09500549  iters=2
...
objno 0 300          <-- AMPL solve_result_num 300 = UNBOUNDED band
#+end_src

It fires after **2 iterations**, at $x=399$ — twelve orders of magnitude short
of the optimum. Meanwhile the *same binary on the same .nl* with
=solver_selection=nlp= returns =EXIT: Optimal Solution Found.=,
$f=-5.0000\times10^{11}$, =objno 0 0=; and Ipopt returns the same correct
value.

** Sweep — the threshold is a hard cut on $|P|$

=2026-07-22_nlp_unbounded_falsepositive_qp.py=, $M=10^2\ldots10^{16}$:

| $M$ (=$x^*$) | pounce auto | pounce nlp | Ipopt |
|--------------+-------------+------------+-------|
| $10^2..10^9$ | SOLVED, exact | SOLVED | SOLVED |
| $10^{10}..10^{16}$ | *UNBOUNDED* | SOLVED, exact | SOLVED |

Fine sweep localizes the cut to exactly $P=1.0\times10^{-10}$:
$P=1.111\times10^{-10}$ → =optimal=; $P=1.000\times10^{-10}$ →
=dual_infeasible=. Confirmed identically through the Python =solve_qp= entry
point, so it is the QP core, not the =.nl= path. Not affected by =tol=1e-12=
or =max_iter=500=.

*** It is not scale-invariant

Multiplying the whole objective by a positive constant $s$ — a mathematically
identical problem with the same minimizer — flips the verdict:

| $s$ | $P=s/M$ | $c=-s$ | status | obj/$s$ |
|-----+---------+--------+--------+---------|
| $1$ | 1e-12 | -1 | *dual_infeasible* | -3.99e+02 |
| $10^3$ | 1e-09 | -1e3 | optimal | -5.000e+11 |
| $10^6$ | 1e-06 | -1e6 | optimal | -5.000e+11 |
| $10^{10}$ | 1e-02 | -1e10 | optimal | -5.000e+11 |

So the trigger is the *absolute* magnitude of $P$, not the $P/c$ ratio: the
unboundedness certificate is not invariant under objective scaling.

* Cross-check / Verification

- *Analytic*: closed-form $x^*,f^*$ derived per instance, $f''>0$ and
  coercivity checked.
- *scipy*: =minimize_scalar= reproduces every Group C $x^*$ to $\le5\times
  10^{-9}$ relative, and confirms $f(10^{10}),f(10^{12}),f(10^{14})>f(x^*)$.
- *Ipopt 3.14.19* on byte-identical =.nl=: correct finite optimum on all of
  Group C, including both false-unbounded cases.
- *POUNCE's own NLP route* (=solver_selection=nlp=) on the same =.nl=: correct.
- *cvxpy oracles*: Clarabel, SCS and OSQP **all also report =unbounded=** on
  $P=10^{-12},c=-1$. This is important and is why the finding is scoped as a
  routing/system defect rather than a defect in the QP core: pounce's convex QP
  IPM behaves exactly like the state of the art for this input.

** Non-findings, recorded for completeness

- *B1, B2* ($-x^{0.1}$, $-\log\log x$): both POUNCE and Ipopt return
  =Optimal=. Since $\nabla f\to0$ along the ray, the KKT residual test is met
  asymptotically and *any* gradient-based solver stops. Shared, pre-existing,
  solver-independent blind spot — not a POUNCE finding. (Consistent with the
  earlier =-log/-sqrt= result.)
- *C1 at $M=10^9,10^{12}$*: both solvers stop at $f=-18.6/-18.8$ vs the true
  $-19.7/-26.6$ (5%/29% off). Identical values from both — again a shared
  flat-gradient termination effect, not a POUNCE finding.
- *Group A, B3*: 4/4 genuine unboundedness detected by both. No regression
  from #250/#253 in the false-negative direction.

* Verdict

=SOLVER_BUG= — a **false "unbounded" status on a provably bounded problem**,
reachable through POUNCE's default entry point.

The QP core's behaviour matches Clarabel/SCS/OSQP, so the component is not
individually at fault. The defect is at the *system* level: POUNCE's
auto-router classifies $\min -x+x^2/(2M)$, $x\ge0$ as a convex QP and hands it
to a solver whose dual-infeasibility certificate is unreliable when
$\|P\|_\infty<10^{-10}$, with no fallback or cross-check — while POUNCE's own
NLP solver, sitting in the same binary, solves it exactly. A user running
=pounce model.nl= as a drop-in Ipopt replacement gets =objno 0 300=
(UNBOUNDED) where Ipopt gets the correct $-5\times10^{11}$.

This matters beyond the toy: a nearly-linear objective carrying a small
quadratic regularization or loss term (economic dispatch, large-scale LP with
a tiny proximal term, ridge terms with small $\lambda$ on large-magnitude
variables) lands in exactly this regime. Because the guard fires at iteration
2 with no diagnostic, a branch-and-bound or trust-region driver consuming the
status would fathom a node that has a perfectly good finite bound.

Suggested remedies (not implemented — adversary does not modify source):
normalize the unboundedness test by $\|c\|$ rather than testing $\|P\|$
absolutely; or, when the convex QP route returns dual-infeasible, re-solve
through the NLP route before emitting an UNBOUNDED status.
