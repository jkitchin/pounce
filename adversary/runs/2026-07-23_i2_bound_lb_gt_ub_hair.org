* [2026-07-23] i2 lb>ub tiny gap — impossible-bound guard (TOLERANCE)
:PROPERTIES:
:FAMILY: qp

:VERDICT: TOLERANCE

min 0.5 x^2, lb=1.0, ub=1.0-gap (lb>ub ⇒ feasible set empty). cvxpy: infeasible
for all gaps. pounce:
  gap>=1e-6  -> primal_infeasible (correct)
  gap=1e-8   -> numerical_failure, x=nan (ungraceful non-answer, but NOT success)
  gap<=1e-10 -> status='optimal', x=[1.0] (violates ub by 1e-10)
The silent-optimal cases violate ub by <= 1e-10, below the ~1e-8 feasibility
tolerance, so treating lb~=ub as a fixed variable is numerically defensible ⇒
borderline TOLERANCE, reported as an observation, not a hard finding. Worth a
glance: the 1e-8 -> numerical_failure/nan transition is a small ungraceful band
between the silent-optimal and correctly-infeasible regimes.
