* Problem
:PROPERTIES:
:FAMILY: qp
:CLASS: convex QP, two active equalities (budget + target return) plus one active upper bound simultaneously
:SOURCE: Markowitz (1952), "Portfolio Selection", J. Finance 7(1); formulation per Boyd & Vandenberghe, Convex Optimization, Sec 4.4.1 (minimum-variance frontier), with box bounds added
:KNOWN_OPTIMAL: none published in closed form for this instance; validated against two independent solvers (cvxpy/Clarabel, scipy SLSQP)
:N_VARIABLES: 4
:N_CONSTRAINTS: 2 equalities + 4 box bounds (one active)
:SELECTED_BY: log-rotation
:COVERAGE_TARGET: - (qp tied-least-tested at 11, last run 2026-08-06)
:END:

\[
\min \tfrac12 w^T \Sigma w \quad \text{s.t.}\quad \mathbf{1}^Tw=1,\ \mu^Tw=0.10,\ 0\le w\le \mathrm{ub}
\]

\(\Sigma=\mathrm{diag}(0.04,0.09,0.16,0.01)\), \(\mu=(0.08,0.12,0.15,0.05)\),
\(\mathrm{ub}=(0.4,0.4,0.4,0.15)\) -- asset 4's cap of 0.15 is set below its
natural (uncapped) optimal weight (\(\approx0.209\)) specifically to force
that bound active on top of the two equalities, exercising the IPM's
combined equality+inequality+bound KKT block.

** False start (documented)
The first version of this script fixed \(\mathrm{ub}=0.4\) uniformly; at
that cap NO bound was active (asset 4 settled at \(w_4=0.209\), all three
solvers agreeing to \(\sim 10^{-11}\)/\(10^{-7}\)) -- a correct pounce
answer, just not the KKT structure this probe targeted. Tightening asset
4's cap to 0.15 (below its natural weight) forced the bound active as
intended; a second false alarm then appeared when the cvxpy oracle's
constraint list still hard-coded ~w <= 0.4~ instead of referencing the
updated ~ub~ array -- a script bug (cvxpy silently solving the OLD,
looser problem), fixed by referencing ~lb~/~ub~ directly in the cvxpy
constraint list. Both are FORMULATION_ERRORs in the adversary script, not
pounce defects; recorded here so a future run doesn't rediscover them.

* Cross-check / Verification
Two independent solvers (cvxpy/Clarabel interior-point conic, scipy SLSQP
active-set SQP) agree with pounce to \(\sim10^{-10}\) objective error and
\(\sim10^{-7}\) primal error, with the intended bound genuinely active.

* Verdict
PASS -- pounce's combined equality+bound QP KKT solve is correct to high
precision against two independently-implemented solvers.
