* Problem
:PROPERTIES:
:FAMILY: socp
:CLASS: robust least-squares (worst-case residual, two summed SOC terms)
:SOURCE: Boyd & Vandenberghe, "Convex Optimization" (2004), sec. 6.4.2, eq. (6.15): worst-case residual = ||A_bar x - b||_2 + rho ||x||_2.
:KNOWN_OPTIMAL: 3.0529974893 (cvxpy CLARABEL reference; confirmed by ECOS and by the worst-case-residual identity)
:N_VARIABLES: 5 (t1, t2, x0, x1, x2); 3 model unknowns x in R^3
:N_CONSTRAINTS: two SOC cone blocks -- ("soc", 1+m=7) and ("soc", 1+n=4)
:END:

With A = A_bar + u and norm-bounded perturbation ||u||_2 <= rho, the worst-case
residual is (B&V 6.15)

  sup_{||u||<=rho} ||(A_bar+u) x - b||_2 = ||A_bar x - b||_2 + rho ||x||_2,

so robust LS is the SOCP

  minimize   t1 + rho t2
  s.t.       ||A_bar x - b||_2 <= t1     (SOC, dim 1+m)
             ||x||_2          <= t2     (SOC, dim 1+n)

Data: m=6, n=3, rho=0.5 (explicit A_bar, b in the script). This is distinct from
the previously tested single-SOC "least squares (0.5773)": here two SOC blocks
are summed in the objective (a robust/regularized formulation).

pounce cone encoding, z = [t1, t2, x0, x1, x2]:
  block 1 (soc 1+m): s1 = (t1, A_bar x - b)  -> G[0,0]=-1; G[1:,2:]=-A_bar; h[1:]=-b
  block 2 (soc 1+n): s2 = (t2, x)            -> G[n1,1]=-1; G[n1+1:,2:]=-I

* Cross-check / Verification
Two independent conic solvers (ECOS, CLARABEL) agree with pounce to ~3e-9 on the
objective. The small x_inf difference (8e-5) reflects the mild flatness of the
objective near the optimum; objective agreement is what matters and is excellent.

Independent formulation check via the worst-case-residual identity (B&V 6.15) at
the pounce solution x*:
  - analytic ||A_bar x* - b|| + rho||x*|| = 3.052997 (equals the objective);
  - Monte-Carlo sampled sup over ||u||_2<=rho of ||(A_bar+u)x*-b|| = 3.023396,
    which is <= the analytic value (as it must be; sampling underestimates the
    sup). This confirms the SOCP is the correct robust-LS reformulation.

* Verdict
PASS -- pounce solves the two-SOC robust least-squares SOCP correctly, matching
ECOS and CLARABEL to ~3e-9 and consistent with the analytic worst-case-residual
identity. No solver defect.
