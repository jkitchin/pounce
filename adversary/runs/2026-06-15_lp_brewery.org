* Problem

  maximize  13 A + 23 B
  subject to
     5 A + 15 B <= 480   (corn)
     4 A +  4 B <= 160   (hops)
    35 A + 20 B <= 1190  (malt)
     A, B >= 0

Closed-form optimum at intersection of corn & malt constraints:
  5A + 15B = 480, 35A + 20B = 1190  =>  A = 12, B = 28.
  profit = 13*12 + 23*28 = 156 + 644 = 800.  (hops also binding: 4*12+4*28=160.)

pounce minimizes c'x with P=None; use c = [-13, -23], so min = -800.
