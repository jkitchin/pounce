#+TITLE: Batch adversary (b) — solve_qp_batch, 10 equality-constrained QPs (distinct P/c/A/b)
#+DATE: 2026-06-09

* Problem properties
- FAMILY: batch (solve_qp_batch)
- SOURCE: synthetic; 10 independent equality-constrained convex QPs, each with its own
  random SPD P, cost c, constraint matrix A (full row-rank) and RHS b.
  RNG seed 424242 (numpy default_rng). P = M Mᵀ + nI symmetrized → SPD.
- BATCH SIZE: 10 items with mixed dims.
- KNOWN_OPTIMAL: equality-only convex QP ⇒ unique optimum from the KKT linear system
  [[P Aᵀ],[A 0]] [x;λ] = [-c; b]  (closed form, solved with numpy.linalg.solve).
- PER-ITEM DIMS (n vars, m eq-constraints):
  [(4,1),(5,2),(6,1),(4,2),(5,1),(6,2),(4,1),(5,2),(6,1),(4,2)].

* Formulation
For each item:  min_x ½ xᵀP x + cᵀx  s.t. A x = b.
KKT stationarity + primal feasibility give the (n+m)×(n+m) saddle system above; with P SPD
and A full row-rank the system is nonsingular and yields the unique x*.
