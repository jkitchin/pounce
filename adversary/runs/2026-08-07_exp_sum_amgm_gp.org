* Problem
:PROPERTIES:
:FAMILY: exp
:CLASS: geometric program, sum objective / product-lower-bound constraint
:SOURCE: AM-GM inequality (Hardy, Littlewood & Polya, "Inequalities" 2nd ed., Thm 9)
:KNOWN_OPTIMAL: 6.0
:N_VARIABLES: 6 (3 log-vars y_i, 3 epigraph t_i)
:N_CONSTRAINTS: 3 Kexp cones (dim 3 each) + 1 nonneg cone row
:SELECTED_BY: log-rotation
:COVERAGE_TARGET: - (coverage-combined.sh skipped per sandbox env adaptations; family-coverage table used instead, exp tied least-tested at 10)
:END:

Minimize $x_1+x_2+x_3$ subject to $x_1 x_2 x_3 \ge 8$, $x_i>0$. By AM-GM,
$x_1+x_2+x_3 \ge 3(x_1x_2x_3)^{1/3} \ge 3\cdot 8^{1/3}=6$, equality at
$x_1=x_2=x_3=2$. Log-transformed ($y_i=\ln x_i$): minimize
$\sum e^{y_i}$ s.t. $\sum y_i \ge \ln 8$, encoded as 3 exponential-cone
epigraphs $(y_i,1,t_i)\in K_{exp}$ plus one affine (nonneg-cone) inequality.
