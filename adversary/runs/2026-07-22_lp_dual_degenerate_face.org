#+TITLE: Adversary Run: Dual-degenerate LP with a non-unique optimal face
#+DATE: 2026-07-22

* Problem
:PROPERTIES:
:FAMILY: lp
:CLASS: dual degeneracy (objective parallel to a facet) + primal degeneracy (duplicated row)
:SOURCE: Bertsimas & Tsitsiklis, /Introduction to Linear Optimization/ (1997), Ch. 3.2 (degeneracy) and Ch. 4.5 / Thm 4.6 (multiplicity of optimal dual solutions <-> primal degeneracy); Chvatal, /Linear Programming/ (1983) Ch. 3. Instance constructed here so the exact rational optimal face is closed-form.
:KNOWN_OPTIMAL: -12 (exact)
:N_VARIABLES: 2
:N_CONSTRAINTS: 4 inequalities + 2 lower bounds
:END:

\begin{align*}
\min_{x\in\mathbb{R}^2}\quad & -3x_1 - 2x_2 \\
\text{s.t.}\quad r_1:\; & 3x_1 + 2x_2 \le 12 \quad\text{(objective is \emph{parallel} to this facet)}\\
r_2:\; & 6x_1 + 4x_2 \le 24 \quad\text{(exact duplicate of } r_1\times 2)\\
r_3:\; & x_1 + x_2 \le 5 \\
r_4:\; & x_1 \le 3 \\
& x_1, x_2 \ge 0
\end{align*}

** Degeneracy structure (why this instance)
- *Dual degeneracy*: $c = -(3,2)$ is parallel to the normal of $r_1$, so the
  optimum is not a point but the edge
  $\mathcal{F} = \{(x_1,(12-3x_1)/2) : 2 \le x_1 \le 3\} = \mathrm{conv}\{(2,3),\,(3,\tfrac32)\}$,
  with $c^\top x = -12$ everywhere on $\mathcal{F}$.
- *Primal degeneracy*: at the vertex $(2,3)$ three constraints ($r_1,r_2,r_3$)
  are active in $\mathbb{R}^2$. Hence the *dual* optimum is also non-unique:
  because $r_2 = 2r_1$, every $(z_1,z_2)\ge 0$ with $z_1 + 2z_2 = 1$ and
  $z_3=z_4=z_{lb}=0$ is an optimal dual, all with dual value $-h^\top z = -12$.

Deliberately distinct from the already-logged =Degenerate transportation LP=
(2026-06-09), which is a primal-degenerate *equality* problem with a *unique*
optimum. Here the primal optimum is a face and the dual is a ray of solutions.

* Grading protocol
Because $x^\star$ is non-unique by construction, =x= equality is *not* graded.
Graded instead:
1. *(a)* objective vs the exact rational optimum and vs both numeric oracles;
2. *(b)* feasibility of the returned $x$;
3. *(c)* $x$ genuinely lies *on the optimal face* ($3x_1+2x_2=12$ and the
   point projects inside the segment $\mathrm{conv}\{(2,3),(3,\frac32)\}$);
4. *(d)* *dual validity*: dual feasibility $z\ge 0$, stationarity
   $c + G^\top z - z_{lb} + z_{ub} = 0$, complementary slackness
   $z_i(h_i-(Gx)_i)=0$, $z_{lb,j}(x_j-l_j)=0$, and zero duality gap
   $-h^\top z + l^\top z_{lb} = c^\top x$.

Known optimal: *-12* (exact).

Exact vertex enumeration over =Fraction= arithmetic recovered the full feasible
vertex set $\{(2,3),(3,\frac32),(0,5),(3,0),(0,0)\}$ and confirmed *two*
optimal vertices $(2,3)$ and $(3,\frac32)$ — i.e. a 1-dimensional optimal face,
exactly as designed.

The three solvers return *three different points*, all optimal — the signature
of dual degeneracy:
- scipy/HiGHS returns the *vertex* $(3, 1.5)$ (simplex terminates at a basis);
- pounce returns the interior point $(2.6017419, 2.0973872)$, i.e. face
  parameter $t = 0.6017$ along the segment;
- cvxpy/CLARABEL returns $(2.5557884, 2.1663174)$, $t \approx 0.556$.

Both interior-point codes land in the *relative interior* of the optimal face,
as IPM theory predicts (convergence to the analytic center of the optimal
face). This is correct behaviour, not disagreement.

* Cross-check / Verification

*Objective.* Three independent oracles agree on $-12$: exact rational vertex
enumeration (zero floating point), HiGHS simplex (exactly $-12$), and CLARABEL.
pounce is within $2.2\times10^{-10}$ relative of all of them.

*Optimal face membership.* The returned $x$ satisfies the face equation
$3x_1+2x_2=12$ to $2.7\times10^{-9}$ and its projection parameter along
$\mathrm{conv}\{(2,3),(3,\frac32)\}$ is $t=0.6017 \in (0,1)$, with distance to
the segment $7.4\times10^{-10}$. So pounce did not merely hit the right
objective value at some infeasible or off-face point — the point is genuinely
in the relative interior of the true optimal edge.

*Dual validity (the sharp test).* pounce returned
$z = (0.387052655,\ 0.306473673,\ 8.80\times10^{-10},\ 5.46\times10^{-10})$,
$z_{lb} = (6.6\times10^{-11},\ 1.1\times10^{-10})$.
This is *not* an arbitrary vector: the theoretical optimal dual set here is
$\{z : z_1 + 2z_2 = 1,\ z \ge 0,\ z_3 = z_4 = 0\}$, and the returned dual has
$\lvert z_1 + 2z_2 - 1\rvert = 2.6\times10^{-11}$ with $z_3, z_4$ at
$\mathcal{O}(10^{-10})$. pounce split the multiplier for the duplicated row
pair $(r_1, r_2)$ as $0.387 + 2(0.306)$ — an interior member of the non-unique
dual family, again the IPM's analytic-center behaviour. All four KKT conditions
(dual feasibility, stationarity, complementary slackness, zero gap) hold at
$\le 10^{-8}$.

Notably, the redundant duplicate row $r_2$ — which makes $G$ rank-deficient in
the active set at $(2,3)$ and is exactly the structure that breaks LICQ /
uniqueness of multipliers — did *not* destabilise the multiplier estimates:
both $z_1$ and $z_2$ came back finite, positive, and summing correctly, rather
than blowing up or one absorbing the whole weight with the other going
negative. 8 iterations, 1.7 ms.

* Verdict

*PASS.* On an LP built to have a genuinely 1-dimensional optimal face (dual
degeneracy) *and* a non-unique dual (primal degeneracy via a duplicated row),
pounce returns the exact optimal value to $2.2\times10^{-10}$ relative, a
strictly feasible point in the relative interior of the true optimal edge, and
a dual that is a valid member of the non-unique optimal dual family — verified
against all four KKT conditions independently of pounce's own =kkt_error=. The
disagreement in $x$ between pounce, HiGHS, and CLARABEL is expected and correct
(each converges to a different point of the same optimal face) and is precisely
why the grading here is on objective + face membership + dual validity rather
than on $x$. No degeneracy-induced failure mode surfaced: no multiplier
blow-up on the rank-deficient active set, no false vertex, no violated
complementary slackness.
