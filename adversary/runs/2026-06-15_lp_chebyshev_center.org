* Problem

Chebyshev center: center of the largest Euclidean ball inscribed in the
polytope P = { x : a_i^T x <= b_i }.  LP form (variables x, r):

  maximize   r
  subject to a_i^T x + ||a_i||_2 * r <= b_i,   i = 1..m
             r >= 0

Concrete polytope: the 3-4-5 right triangle with vertices (0,0), (4,0), (0,3).
Edges (interior = triangle):

   -x        <= 0     (x >= 0),        ||a|| = 1
       -y    <= 0     (y >= 0),        ||a|| = 1
   3x + 4y   <= 12    (hypotenuse),    ||a|| = 5

For a triangle the Chebyshev center is the incenter and r is the inradius.
3-4-5 right triangle: inradius r = (3 + 4 - 5)/2 = 1; right angle at the origin
with legs on the axes => incenter = (r, r) = (1, 1).

Closed-form optimum: r* = 1, center* = (1, 1).
pounce minimizes c'z (P=None) over z=[x,y,r] with c=[0,0,-1] => min = -1.
