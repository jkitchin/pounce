#+TITLE: Adversary Run: Badly-scaled LP (row x1e8, column x1e-8)
#+DATE: 2026-07-22

* Problem
:PROPERTIES:
:FAMILY: lp
:CLASS: ill-conditioned / mismatched row-column scaling, unique nondegenerate vertex
:SOURCE: Constructed instance in the style of the badly-scaled NETLIB LPs (pilot/scagr/bnl); scaling pathology per Nocedal & Wright, "Numerical Optimization" 2e, S13.5 (Presolve and Scaling), and Gill/Murray/Wright, "Practical Optimization" S8.7 (diagonal equilibration). Optimum derived in closed form by construction.
:KNOWN_OPTIMAL: -24 (min form); x* = (1,2,1); duals = (1,2,3)
:N_VARIABLES: 3
:N_CONSTRAINTS: 3 inequality rows + 3 lower bounds
:END:

Base LP (well-scaled):

\begin{align*}
\max\;& 8x_1 + 3x_2 + 10x_3\\
\text{s.t.}\;& x_1 + x_2 + x_3 \le 4\\
& 2x_1 + x_2 \le 4\\
& x_1 + 3x_3 \le 4\\
& x \ge 0
\end{align*}

$A=[[1,1,1],[2,1,0],[1,0,3]]$ is nonsingular ($\det=-4$) and $x^\*=(1,2,1)$
satisfies $Ax^\*=b=(4,4,4)$ with all three rows active and $x^\*>0$.  The cost
was *built* as $c=A^\top\lambda$ with $\lambda=(1,2,3)>0$, so $\lambda$ is the
unique dual, strict complementarity holds, and $x^\*$ is the unique
nondegenerate optimal vertex.  $z^\*_{\max}=24$, i.e. $-24$ in pounce's
minimization form, and pounce's convention $c+G^\top z-z_{lb}=0$ requires
$z=(1,2,3)$.

Scaled variants (identical LP, invariant optimum -- that is the whole test):

| # | row scaling $R$      | col scaling $S$ (via $x=Su$) | expected $u^\*$ | expected $z$          |
|---+----------------------+------------------------------+-----------------+-----------------------|
| 1 | $(1,1,1)$            | $(1,1,1)$                    | $(1,2,1)$       | $(1,2,3)$             |
| 2 | $(10^8,1,1)$         | $(1,1,1)$                    | $(1,2,1)$       | $(10^{-8},2,3)$       |
| 3 | $(10^8,1,1)$         | $(10^{-8},1,1)$              | $(10^8,2,1)$    | $(10^{-8},2,3)$       |
| 4 | $(10^8,1,10^{-6})$   | $(10^{-8},1,1)$              | $(10^8,2,1)$    | $(10^{-8},2,3\cdot10^6)$ |

Variant 4 spreads the row scaling over 14 orders of magnitude and the column
scaling over 8, giving a matrix whose entries span $10^{-8}$ to $10^{8}$.

Known optimal: -24 (all four variants; relative errors above are against the
exact-rational vertex enumeration on the same float data).

Objective spread across the four scalings: 2.47e-07.  Worst relative error over
all variants and all reported quantities (primal, dual, objective): 2.08e-07.

pounce recovered the *same* optimum in all four variants, including the exact
scaling-transformed primal ($u_1^\*=10^8$) and the exact scaling-transformed
row duals ($z_1=10^{-8}$, $z_3=3\times10^6$).  So pounce's internal scaling is
transparent: it does not leak into the reported objective, primal or duals.
Cost of the pathology is iteration count (8 -> 21 -> 36), not accuracy.

Reported dual sign convention is confirmed to be $c+G^\top z - z_{lb}=0$ with
$z\ge 0$ for $Gx\le h$ (matches HiGHS's negated =ineqlin.marginals= and
CLARABEL's constraint dual, both cross-checked componentwise).  The scaled
stationarity residual at pounce's returned point is 1.4e-10 (base) and
2.1e-16 (variant 4).

* Cross-check / Verification

1. *Exact vertex enumeration* (Fractions, all $\binom{6}{3}=20$ basis
   candidates, run on the exact float data pounce receives) -- ground truth,
   zero roundoff.  Returns $-24$ in every variant.  pounce agrees to <= 1.0e-8.
2. *scipy.optimize.linprog (HiGHS)* -- agrees on variants 1-3 to <= 1e-10.
   *On variant 4 HiGHS is wrong*: it returns obj $=-29.3333$ at
   $u=(2\times10^8,0,4/3)$, which violates row 3 by 2.0 in unscaled units
   (2e-6 after the $10^{-6}$ row scaling, i.e. below its internal tolerance
   after its own presolve/equilibration).  pounce's max unscaled row violation
   on the same instance is 0.0.  Verified by direct residual evaluation in the
   script (=unscaled max row violation= lines).  This is an *oracle* failure,
   not a pounce failure.
3. *cvxpy / CLARABEL* (independent IPM) -- agrees with pounce and with the
   exact enumeration on all four variants (worst obj deviation 2.0e-06 on
   variant 4, i.e. CLARABEL is less accurate there than pounce).

* Verdict

*PASS.*  pounce solves all four scalings of the same LP to the correct
objective, the correct scaling-transformed vertex, and the correct
scaling-transformed duals, with worst relative error 2.1e-07 even when the
constraint matrix entries span $10^{-8}$ to $10^{8}$.  Internal scaling is
observably transparent -- the objective spread across scalings is 2.5e-07 and
the duals come back in the exact $\lambda/R$ form theory predicts, in the
documented sign convention.  The only degradation is iteration count (8 -> 36).
Notably, on the most extreme variant the usual reference oracle
(scipy/HiGHS) returned an infeasible point with a spuriously better objective,
while pounce and CLARABEL both stayed correct; this is worth remembering when
HiGHS is used as an LP oracle on badly-scaled data.
