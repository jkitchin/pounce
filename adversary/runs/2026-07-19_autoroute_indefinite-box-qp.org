#+DATE: <2026-07-19>

* Problem
:PROPERTIES:
:FAMILY: autoroute
:CLASS: nonconvex (indefinite) quadratic objective + box bounds
:SOURCE: Classic indefinite BoxQP; Nocedal & Wright, /Numerical Optimization/ 2e, Ch. 16.1 (an indefinite quadratic on a box has its minimizers on the boundary; the interior stationary point is a saddle)
:KNOWN_OPTIMAL: A: -1.0 at (1,-1)/(-1,1);  B: -5e-4 at (0,±1)
:N_VARIABLES: 2
:N_CONSTRAINTS: 4 bounds (box), no general constraints
:END:

Case A (clearly indefinite, eigenvalues 3 and -1):
\begin{align*}
\min\quad & \tfrac12(x_1^2+x_2^2) + 2x_1x_2, \qquad -1\le x\le 1
\end{align*}
The interior stationary point $(0,0)$ is a *saddle* ($f=0$). Vertex values:
$(1,1)\to3$, $(-1,-1)\to3$, $(1,-1)\to-1$, $(-1,1)\to-1$, so $f^\*=-1$.

Case B (barely indefinite, $\lambda_{\min}=-10^{-3}$):
\begin{align*}
\min\quad & \tfrac12 x_1^2 - \tfrac12\cdot 10^{-3}\, x_2^2, \qquad -1\le x\le 1
\end{align*}
$f^\* = -5\times10^{-4}$ at $(0,\pm1)$. This is the tolerance trap: a convexity
test with a sloppy threshold would classify this as a convex QP and return
$x=(0,0)$, $f=0$ — a *wrong answer*, not merely a slower route.

The routing contract under test: =solver_selection="auto"= must agree with
forced =solver_selection="nlp"=, and must **not** hand either problem to the
convex LP/QP or SOCP fast path (both assume a PSD Hessian).

Run: =adversary/runs/2026-07-19_autoroute_indefinite-box-qp.py=

=auto= vs forced =nlp= objective gap: **0.0e+00 in both cases** (bit-identical
x as well). In both cases =res.info.get("solver")= is =None=, i.e. no
specialized convex route was taken — the correct outcome, since a PSD-assuming
route on an indefinite Hessian would be a genuine wrong answer, not a
conservative fall-through.

Both cases land on a *global* minimizer (A: (1,-1); B: (≈0, ≈1)), matching the
brute-force grid and scipy from the same x0. The small residual (1e-8 in A,
2.5e-9 in B) is the interior-point solver's bound-relaxation distance from the
box boundary, not a routing effect.

* Cross-check / Verification
- Gradient finite-differenced against the analytic =gf= before every solve
  (assert in the script).
- Global oracle: 401×401 dense grid over the box for both cases — reproduces
  f* = -1 at (1,-1) and f* = -5e-4 at (0,-1).
- Local oracle from the same x0: =scipy.optimize.minimize(method="L-BFGS-B",
  tol=1e-12)= — agrees to 1.5e-08 (A) and 2.5e-09 (B).
- cvxpy is deliberately *not* used: the problems are nonconvex and would be
  rejected as non-DCP; using it would be a formulation error.
- Routing transparency: =auto= and forced =nlp= produced identical objectives
  and identical x to all printed digits.

* Verdict
=PASS= — the auto-router correctly declines to specialize on an indefinite
quadratic, including the barely-indefinite case B where the min eigenvalue is
only -1e-3 and a loose PSD tolerance would have produced x=(0,0), f=0 instead
of f=-5e-4. Answers under =auto= and forced =nlp= agree exactly, so the
routing-transparency contract holds. No =ROUTING_ERROR=; the fall-through to
the NLP solver is the correct (and here the only sound) route.
