* Problem
:PROPERTIES:
:FAMILY: power
:CLASS: power-cone epigraph on an affine residual (regression, not a bare p-norm-of-x constraint)
:SOURCE: Boyd & Vandenberghe, "Convex Optimization" (2004) Sec 6.1.1 (norm approximation); MOSEK Modeling Cookbook Sec 3.2.4 ("Power cone", p-norm reduction)
:KNOWN_OPTIMAL: none published for this instance (oracle-only)
:N_VARIABLES: 16 (3 fit vars + 6 residuals + 6 epigraph vars + 1 shared aux)
:N_CONSTRAINTS: 7 equalities, 6x power cone (alpha=1/1.5)
:SELECTED_BY: log-rotation
:COVERAGE_TARGET: -
:END:

\[
\min_x \; \|Ax-b\|_p, \qquad p=1.5,\quad A\in\mathbb{R}^{6\times3}
\]

encoded per-residual as \((r_i, u_i, w)\) in a 3-D power cone with
\(\alpha=1/p\), \(w\equiv1\), minimizing \(\sum_i u_i\) (equivalent to
minimizing \(\|r\|_p^p\), whose minimizer coincides with the \(p\)-norm
minimizer for fixed \(p\)).
