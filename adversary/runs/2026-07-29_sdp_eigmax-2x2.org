#+TITLE: Adversary Run: Maximize min-eigenvalue of a 2x2 affine matrix pencil
#+DATE: 2026-07-29

* Problem
:PROPERTIES:
:FAMILY: sdp
:CLASS: eigenvalue-optimization SDP (Boyd & Vandenberghe sec 3.1.5 / 4.6.3 style)
:SOURCE: A(x) = A0 + x*A1, A0=[[2,1],[1,3]], A1=[[1,0],[0,-1]]. lambda_min(A(x))
  is concave in x (pointwise min of v^TA(x)v over unit v). Closed form for a
  2x2 symmetric matrix [[a,b],[b,d]]: lambda_min=(a+d)/2 - sqrt(((a-d)/2)^2+b^2).
  Here a+d=5 (constant), giving f(x)=2.5-sqrt((x-0.5)^2+1), maximized at x*=0.5.
  Verified independently on a 20001-point grid via numpy.linalg.eigvalsh before
  running any solver.
:KNOWN_OPTIMAL: x*=0.5, t*=lambda_min(A(x*))=1.5
:N_VARIABLES: 2 (x, t)
:N_CONSTRAINTS: 1 psd cone, n=2 (svec dim 3)
:SELECTED_BY: log-rotation
:COVERAGE_TARGET: - (coverage-combined.sh skipped per environment adaptation; sdp was the next-least-tested family after batch/lp/qp/qp-active-set/exp/power/autoroute in adversary/log.org's Family-coverage table, Tested=7)
:END:

$$\max_{x,t} t \quad \text{s.t.} \quad A_0 + x A_1 - t I \succeq 0$$

Encoded via a single 2x2 PSD cone: $s = \mathrm{svec}(A(x) - tI) =
(2+x-t,\; \sqrt2,\; 3-x-t)$ (off-diagonal entry is constant, independent of
$x$, so its svec row has zero coefficients on the variables).
