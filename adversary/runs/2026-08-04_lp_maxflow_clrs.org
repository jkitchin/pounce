Maximize flow out of source $s$ on the CLRS Figure 26.1 network
($s, v_1, v_2, v_3, v_4, t$; 9 directed edges with given capacities),
formulated directly as an LP:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\text{maximize} \quad & f(s,v_1) + f(s,v_2) \\
\text{subject to} \quad & 0 \le f_e \le \text{cap}_e \quad \forall e \\
& \text{flow conservation at } v_1, v_2, v_3, v_4
\end{aligned}
\]

Known optimal: 23.0 (CLRS worked example)

pounce's objective matches both the closed-form known max-flow value and the
independent scipy HiGHS LP oracle to ~1.7e-11 relative error. The edge-flow
*vector* differs between pounce and HiGHS by up to 1.11 in max-norm
(``s_v1``/``v2_v1`` split differently) -- this is expected primal
degeneracy: a balanced flow network's maximum flow has multiple valid edge
decompositions achieving the same flow value, and interior-point vs. simplex
methods land on different vertices/interior points of the optimal face. Both
solutions independently satisfy capacity bounds and conservation equalities
to ~1e-12, confirming both are genuinely optimal, not that either is wrong.
