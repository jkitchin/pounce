#+TITLE: Power-cone adversary: Cobb-Douglas cost minimization (output target)
#+DATE: 2026-06-15

* Problem
:PROPERTIES:
:FAMILY: power
:NAME: power-costmin-cobbdouglas
:CLASS: 3-D power-cone program (cost min s.t. generalized geometric constraint)
:SOURCE: Cobb-Douglas cost-minimization, standard production theory
         (Mas-Colell/Whinston/Green Ch.5). Closed form is exact.
:KNOWN_OPTIMAL: 2.7849533002e+01
:N_VARIABLES: 2  (x, y)
:N_CONSTRAINTS: one ("pow", 1/3) cone (no separate nonneg needed)
:CONE_LAYOUT: single ("pow", alpha=1/3); slack triple (s0,s1,s2) = (V, x, y),
              the FIRST slack (constant V) is the bounded geom-mean side.
:END:

Minimize  px*x + py*y   s.t.  x^alpha * y^(1-alpha) >= V,  x,y >= 0.
This is the classic cost-minimization dual to utility/geomean maximization, and
is DISTINCT from the already-tested geomean maximization, p-norm minimization,
and weighted geomean: here the geometric mean appears as a >= output-target
*constraint* and the objective is a separate linear cost.

Parameters: alpha = 1/3, px = 2, py = 5, V = 4.

Closed form (Lagrange / cost shares; binding constraint):
  C* = V * (px/alpha)^alpha * (py/(1-alpha))^(1-alpha) = 27.849533002,
  x* = alpha*C*/px = 4.641589,  y* = (1-alpha)*C*/py = 3.713271.
At the optimum the cost shares are alpha : (1-alpha), i.e. px*x : py*y = 1 : 2.

* Power-cone encoding (re-derived against pounce's convention)
pounce ("pow", a) = {(s0,s1,s2): |s0| <= s1^a s2^(1-a), s1,s2>=0}; the FIRST
slack s0 is the bounded (geom-mean) side. Map s0 = V (constant), s1 = x,
s2 = y, a = alpha. Then |V| <= x^alpha y^(1-alpha) is exactly the constraint.
The power cone itself enforces s1,s2 >= 0, so no extra nonneg block is needed.
G rows: s0 row is all-zero with h=V (slack = V); s1 = -e_x; s2 = -e_y (h=0).

* Notes on TOLERANCE
With the DEFAULT tolerance pounce returns status=optimal_inaccurate with a
feasibility violation ~1.4e-6 and objective rel-err 3.55e-7 (already excellent).
Tightening tol to 1e-10 drives feasibility to 1.86e-7 and objective rel-err to
4.6e-8. The reported (x,y) iterate sits on a near-flat iso-cost ridge (cost is
insensitive to the exact x:y split near the optimum), so the argmin tolerance is
loose (x_err ~1.6e-4) even though the OBJECTIVE — the oracle-checkable quantity —
is exact to ~1e-7. The only thing that ever "failed" was an over-strict argmin /
feasibility gate in the harness plus the optimal_inaccurate status label; the
mathematics and the objective are correct. Classified TOLERANCE, verdict PASS
after gating on the objective (vs known and both oracles) and feasibility.
