#+TITLE: Adversary Run: Extreme-coefficient geometric program (exp cone)
#+DATE: 2026-07-23

* Problem
:PROPERTIES:
:FAMILY: exp
:CLASS: extreme-scale GP
:SOURCE: min x + K/x (x>0); AM-GM optimum x*=sqrt(K), f*=2 sqrt(K). K=1e12 => f*=2e6.
:KNOWN_OPTIMAL: 2.0e6
:N_VARIABLES: 3 (u,t1,t2)
:N_CONSTRAINTS: two 3-D exponential cones
:END:

min x + K/x, modeled with x=e^u:  min t1+t2 s.t. (u,1,t1)∈Kexp, (lnK−u,1,t2)∈Kexp.
Optimal cone variables t1=t2=sqrt(K)=1e6.

* Results
:PROPERTIES:
:STATUS: FAIL
:END:

| Solver          | Status            | Objective      | Rel Err vs f* | Wall Time |
|-----------------+-------------------+----------------+---------------+-----------|
| pounce (exp)    | numerical_failure | 1.99999025e6   | 4.87e-6       | 0.009 s   |
| cvxpy ECOS      | optimal           | 2.00000000e6   | 9.7e-10       | 0.003 s   |
| cvxpy SCS       | inaccurate        | 7.286e5        | 6.4e-1 (fail) | —         |

Known optimal: 2.0e6.

pounce returns an objective correct to 4.87e-6 but reports =numerical_failure=
(success=False). ECOS solves the identical model to 9.7e-10; SCS diverges (bad
oracle here — ECOS is the reliable one).

* Cross-check / Verification
Residuals at the returned point (K=1e12):
- primal_infeasibility = 4.9e-6  (feasible)
- dual_infeasibility   = 5.4e-8  (dual-feasible)
- complementarity      = 3.84    (= reported kkt_error)
- scaling_warning      = None

The reported =kkt_error= is the raw complementarity gap s·z, measured in
ABSOLUTE units. The optimal cone variables are ~1e6, so the complementarity
floor is ~1e6 × (relative tol) ≈ 1, which never reaches the absolute default
tolerance ⇒ =numerical_failure=. Normalizing the gap (÷(1+|obj|)) gives
1.9e-6, well inside tol.

Controls confirming root cause:
- =tol=1e-6= ⇒ status flips to =optimal= at the *same* iterate (no re-solve).
- =max_iter= up to 50000 ⇒ no change (it is an accuracy plateau, not an iter cap).
- Hand-rescaled equivalent (substitute x=sqrt(K)·w so optimal cone vars are O(1),
  obj = sqrt(K)(t1+t2)) ⇒ =optimal=, rel err 2.5e-7.
- K sweep: optimal for K≤1e10; numerical_failure for K≥1e12 (obj still correct;
  rel err grows 5e-6 → 8.7e-5 as K:1e12→1e14).

* Verdict
SOLVER_LIMITATION — pounce's non-symmetric HSDE (exp/power) driver uses an
unnormalized complementarity gap as its convergence/status criterion. On a
well-posed GP whose optimal cone variables are large (extreme-but-legitimate
coefficient scaling), the absolute complementarity cannot reach the absolute
tolerance, so the solver reports =numerical_failure= / success=False at a point
that is primal-feasible (4.9e-6), dual-feasible (5.4e-8), and objective-correct
(4.87e-6). ECOS solves it to 1e-9; a hand-rescaled equivalent and =tol=1e-6=
both recover =optimal=. =scaling_warning= is not raised. Same defect confirmed
in the power cone (see companion run). Analogous in spirit to the scale-
normalization issues #324 (QP) / #327 (NLP), here in the HSDE conic driver.
Filed jointly with the power case.
