* Cross-check / Verification
Eigenvalues of $Z^\top P Z$ are invariant to the choice of orthonormal
basis $Z$ for a fixed null space (any two such bases are related by an
orthogonal $Q$, and $Z_2^\top P Z_2 = Q^\top(Z_1^\top P Z_1)Q$ is an
orthogonal similarity transform, exactly spectrum-preserving) — so the
eigenvalue comparison is exact, not approximate, regardless of pounce's
internal basis choice vs numpy's SVD-derived basis. Max abs eigenvalue
error: $4.44\times10^{-16}$ (machine epsilon). $x^*$ additionally confirmed
against cvxpy/CLARABEL (fully independent solver) to $1.0\times10^{-8}$.
