#+TITLE: Adversary Run: Nearest Correlation Matrix (rank-deficient SDP optimum)
#+DATE: 2026-07-19

* Problem
:PROPERTIES:
:FAMILY: sdp
:CLASS: projection onto the elliptope; optimum on the PSD boundary, X* singular (rank 2 of 3), non-strictly-complementary
:SOURCE: N. J. Higham, "Computing the nearest correlation matrix -- a problem from finance", IMA J. Numer. Anal. 22(3):329-343, 2002 (Secs. 1-3); MOSEK Modeling Cookbook v3.3, Sec. 6.2 "nearest correlation matrix"
:KNOWN_OPTIMAL: sqrt(6)*0.3 = 0.7348469228349534 (exact, derived by symmetry)
:N_VARIABLES: 4 (epigraph T + 3 free off-diagonals)
:N_CONSTRAINTS: SOC(4) + PSD(3) [svec length 6]; unit-diagonal equalities substituted out
:END:

\begin{align*}
\text{minimize} \quad & \| X - A \|_F \\
\text{subject to} \quad & \operatorname{diag}(X) = \mathbf 1,\qquad X \succeq 0
\end{align*}

with the equicorrelation input (a symmetric, unit-diagonal, but *indefinite*
correlation estimate -- exactly Higham's motivating pathology)
\[
A = I + \rho(J - I),\quad \rho = -0.8,\ n = 3,\qquad
\operatorname{eig}(A) = \{1+2\rho,\ 1-\rho,\ 1-\rho\} = \{-0.6,\ 1.8,\ 1.8\}.
\]

** Exact optimum by symmetry
The elliptope \(\mathcal E_3 = \{X \succeq 0,\ \operatorname{diag}X = \mathbf 1\}\)
and the Frobenius objective are invariant under \(X \mapsto PXP^\top\) for any
permutation \(P\), and \(A\) is permutation invariant. Projection onto a closed
convex set is unique, so the minimizer is itself permutation invariant:
\(X = I + t(J-I)\). For that family \(\operatorname{eig}(X) = \{1+2t, 1-t, 1-t\}\),
so \(X \succeq 0 \iff t \in [-1/2, 1]\), and \(\|X-A\|_F^2 = 6(t-\rho)^2\).
Minimizing \(6(t+0.8)^2\) over \([-1/2,1]\) clamps at the boundary:
\[
t^\star = -\tfrac12,\quad X^\star = I - \tfrac12(J-I),\quad
\operatorname{eig}(X^\star) = \{0,\ \tfrac32,\ \tfrac32\},\quad
\text{OPT} = \sqrt6 \cdot 0.3 = 0.7348469228 .
\]
\(X^\star\) is *singular* -- the optimum sits on the boundary of the PSD cone
with a zero eigenvalue and a rank-1 dual, so there is no strictly complementary
pair and the central path converges to a non-interior limit. This is the
degeneracy the run was chosen to probe; the five prior =sdp= runs (max-eig,
min-trace, max-cut K3, trace-1 min-eig, Lyapunov) all had well-conditioned,
strictly complementary optima.

pounce returns \(X\) matching \(X^\star\) to 1.1e-09 entrywise, with
\(\operatorname{eig}(X) = \{0,\ 1.5,\ 1.5\}\) and a minimum eigenvalue of
\(-2\times10^{-11}\) -- it lands on the singular face without any negative
eigenvalue blow-up, which is the non-trivial part on a degenerate SDP. The
epigraph is tight: \(T - \|X-A\|_F = -3.0\times10^{-9}\).

* Cross-check / Verification
- *svec layout re-derived and numerically verified before judging pounce.* The
  documented convention is: lower triangle, column by column, off-diagonals
  scaled by \(\sqrt2\), length \(n(n+1)/2\). For \(n=3\) that is
  \(\operatorname{svec}(X) = [X_{00},\ \sqrt2 X_{10},\ \sqrt2 X_{20},\ X_{11},\ \sqrt2 X_{21},\ X_{22}]\).
  The script asserts on 5 random symmetric pairs that
  \(\langle X,Y\rangle = \operatorname{svec}(X)\cdot\operatorname{svec}(Y)\) and
  \(\|\operatorname{svec}(X)\|_2 = \|X\|_F\) *before* the solve; both hold to
  1e-10. This also justifies folding the objective into a plain SOC: because
  the diagonals of \(X\) and \(A\) match exactly,
  \(\|X-A\|_F^2 = 2\sum_i (u_i-\rho)^2\), so
  \(\mathrm{SOC}(4) = \{(T, \sqrt2(u-\rho\mathbf 1))\}\) is the exact epigraph.
- Cone rows follow \(s = h - Gz\) with \(z = (T, u_0, u_1, u_2)\):
  PSD block \(h = [1,0,0,1,0,1]\), \(G[1,1] = G[2,2] = G[4,3] = -\sqrt2\),
  reproducing \(\operatorname{svec}(X(u))\) exactly.
- Two independent cvxpy solvers (CLARABEL, SCS) on the *unreduced* DCP model
  (full symmetric matrix variable, =X >> 0=, =cp.diag(X) == 1=,
  =cp.norm(X - A, 'fro')=), i.e. neither the symmetry reduction nor the
  diagonal substitution is shared with the oracle. Both agree with pounce and
  with the analytic value; SCS's 1.3e-06 deviation is its own first-order
  accuracy.
- The analytic \(X^\star\) is independently checked in-script:
  \(\|X^\star - A\|_F = \sqrt6\cdot0.3\) to 1e-14 and
  \(\lambda_{\min}(X^\star) = 0\) to 1e-14.

* Verdict
PASS -- on a deliberately degenerate SDP whose optimum is a singular (rank-2)
matrix on the PSD boundary with no strictly complementary solution, pounce
recovers the exact analytic optimum to 3.1e-10 relative and the exact minimizer
to 1.1e-09 entrywise, with a clean =optimal= status and no negative-eigenvalue
leakage. The svec encoding was re-derived and verified numerically before any
comparison. Two independent cvxpy solvers confirm. No finding.
