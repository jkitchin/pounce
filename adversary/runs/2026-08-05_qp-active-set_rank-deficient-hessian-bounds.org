:SELECTED_BY: log-rotation

\begin{align}
\min\quad & x_1^2 + x_2^2 - 4x_1 - 6x_2 - x_3 \\
\text{s.t.}\quad & -5 \le x_1,x_2,x_3 \le 5
\end{align}

\(P=\mathrm{diag}(2,2,0)\) is positive SEMIdefinite with a genuine zero
eigenvalue (flat direction along \(x_3\)); \(f\) is unbounded below in
\(x_3\) without the box bound. By inspection the unconstrained-in-\(x_1,
x_2\) minimizer is \((2,3)\) (interior to \([-5,5]\)), and \(f\) decreases
linearly in \(x_3\) until the upper bound is hit, giving \(x^*=(2,3,5)\),
\(f^*=(4-8)+(9-18)+(-5)=-18\), with exactly one active bound.
