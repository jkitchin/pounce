* Problem
:PROPERTIES:
:FAMILY: qp
:CLASS: degeneracy / constraint-qualification failure / non-unique optimum
:SOURCE: Constructed. Nocedal & Wright, /Numerical Optimization/ 2e, Ch. 16.1-16.2 (equality QP, null-space method, singular reduced Hessian) + Ch. 16.8 (rank-deficient/redundant constraint Jacobian); Boyd & Vandenberghe, /Convex Optimization/, Sec. 4.4 (PSD-P QP: optimal set is affine).
:KNOWN_OPTIMAL: p* = -13 (singular-P cases), p* = -1.9479166666667 (SPD control case) -- exact, by the reduced null-space method
:N_VARIABLES: 5
:N_CONSTRAINTS: 2 / 4 / 8 equality rows, all of rank 2
:END:

\begin{align*}
\min_{x\in\mathbb{R}^5}\quad & \tfrac12 x^\top P x + c^\top x, \qquad
P = M^\top M,\; M=\begin{bmatrix}1&1&0&0&0\\0&0&1&1&0\end{bmatrix}\\
\text{s.t.}\quad & Ax=b
\end{align*}

with \(c=(1,2,3,4,-1)\). \(\operatorname{rank}(P)=2\), nullity 3 — \(P\) is
positive *semi*-definite and structurally singular.

Base equality rows:
\[
a_1=(1,0,1,0,1),\;b_1=3 \qquad a_2=(0,1,0,1,1),\;b_2=2
\]
Redundant rows appended: \(a_3=a_1\) (exact duplicate), \(a_4=2a_1-a_2\), and in
the stress case also \(10^6 a_1\), \(-3a_2\), \(a_1+a_2\), \(10^{-6}a_2\) — so
\(A\) is up to \(8\times5\) with \(\operatorname{rank}(A)=2\). LICQ fails
everywhere on the feasible set; the multiplier \(y\) is non-unique.

** The optimal set
\[
\mathcal N(P)\cap\mathcal N(A)=\operatorname{span}\{d\},\quad d=(1,-1,-1,1,0),
\qquad c^\top d = 1-2-3+4 = 0,
\]
so the QP is bounded below and its solution set is a **line**
\(X^\star = x_{\mathrm{ref}} + t\,d\), not a point. Grading is therefore on
objective + feasibility + *membership*, never on \(x\) equality:

1. \(|f(x)-p^\star|/\max(1,|p^\star|) < 10^{-6}\)
2. \(\|Ax-b\|_\infty < 10^{-6}\)
3. \(\|Z^\top(Px+c)\|_\infty < 10^{-6}\), \(Z\) an SVD basis of \(\mathcal N(A)\)
   (vanishing reduced gradient \(\iff\) \(x\in X^\star\), since the reduced
   problem is convex)

Three attribution cases isolate the two defects, plus one stress case:
| case        | P          | A                        |
|-------------+------------+--------------------------|
| =semidef_P= | singular   | 2x5, full row rank       |
| =dup_rows=  | SPD (P+I)  | 4x5, rank 2              |
| =both=      | singular   | 4x5, rank 2              |
| =stress8x5= | singular   | 8x5, rank 2, scaled 1e±6 |

* Cross-check / Verification
- *Analytic oracle.* \(x_{\mathrm{ref}}\), \(p^\star\) computed by the exact
  null-space method: min-norm particular point \(x_p = A^{+}b\), \(Z\) from the
  SVD of \(A\), reduced Hessian \(Z^\top P Z\) (rank 2 of 3 — itself singular),
  reduced step by =lstsq= (min-norm). Independent of every solver.
- *cvxpy CLARABEL and OSQP* both agree with the analytic \(p^\star\) to ~1e-15
  in all four cases; =cp.psd_wrap= is used so cvxpy does not reject the
  numerically-singular \(P\) as non-PSD.
- *Optimal-set membership.* Beyond matching the objective, the pounce point's
  reduced gradient \(Z^\top(Px+c)\) vanishes to 2.7e-10 or better, so pounce
  lands *on* \(X^\star\), not merely near it in objective.
- *Direction decomposition.* \(x_{\mathrm{pounce}}-x_{\mathrm{ref}}\) was split
  along vs. orthogonal to the optimal-set direction \(d\). The orthogonal
  component is 3.9e-10 (=semidef_P=, =both=) and 4.8e-15 (=stress8x5=) while the
  total displacement is 1.6e-7 to 1.6e-6 — i.e. pounce's answer differs from the
  analytic reference *only by a slide along the optimal line*, exactly as a
  correct solver on a non-unique optimum should. This is the check that would
  have caught a solver silently drifting off the optimal set.
- *Attribution.* The =semidef_P= and =dup_rows= cases isolate the two defects
  separately; both pass, so neither the singular KKT (2,2)-block nor the
  rank-deficient \(A\) alone is a failure mode, and the combined =both= case
  passes too. No case needed non-default =tol= or =max_iter=.
- No spurious =infeasible= / =unbounded= status, no NaN, no iteration-limit hit.
  Total script runtime 0.81 s wall (all 4 cases + 8 oracle solves).

* Verdict
*PASS.* pounce's convex QP IPM handles a structurally singular KKT system — a
positive-*semi*-definite rank-2 Hessian in a 5-variable problem combined with an
equality Jacobian of up to 8 rows and rank 2 (exact duplicates, linear
combinations, and rows scaled by \(10^{\pm6}\)) — without any regularization
artifact, spurious infeasibility, or status error. It returns a point that is
genuinely a member of the one-dimensional optimal set: objective matches the
exact \(p^\star=-13\) to 1.4e-11 or better, feasibility to ~1e-10, and the
reduced gradient (the membership certificate) to 2.7e-10. The residual
displacement from the analytic reference point lies almost entirely along the
optimal-set direction \(d=(1,-1,-1,1,0)\), with an orthogonal component of only
3.9e-10, confirming the solver is picking a legitimate alternative optimum
rather than drifting. LICQ fails throughout (the multipliers are non-unique) and
this causes no observable trouble. pounce is also the fastest of the three
solvers on every case, and the most accurate on the badly-scaled stress case.
