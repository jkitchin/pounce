* Key confirmations
- *Misrouting resistance.* For the two nonconvex traps (indefinite objective with
  LINEAR inequalities; concave QCQP with a nonconvex feasible region) the router
  CONSERVATIVELY fell through to the general NLP backend rather than sending them
  to a convex qp/socp solver. Both reached the correct global optimum (0 and -2).
  No convex-relaxation artifact.
- *Correct specialization.* Genuinely convex shapes were specialized correctly:
  the ball to =socp=, the degenerate (redundant-row) LP to =lp-ipm=, and the
  rank-deficient PSD QP to =qp-ipm= — each matching the known optimum to 1e-9 or
  better.
- *Routing transparency.* =auto= and forced =nlp= agree in objective (<=5.8e-9)
  and solution (<=2.9e-9) in every case. No ROUTING_ERROR.

* Verdict
PASS — the router neither misroutes nonconvex problems to convex solvers nor
