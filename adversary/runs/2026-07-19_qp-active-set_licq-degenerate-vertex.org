* Problem
:PROPERTIES:
:FAMILY: qp-active-set
:CLASS: primal/dual degenerate convex QP (LICQ failure at the optimum)
:SOURCE: Standard degenerate-QP construction; Nocedal & Wright, /Numerical Optimization/ 2e, Ch. 16.5 ("Degeneracy"/cycling in active-set QP) and Ch. 12.6 (LICQ)
:KNOWN_OPTIMAL: 1.0 at x* = (1,1)
:N_VARIABLES: 2
:N_CONSTRAINTS: 3 inequalities (all active at x*)
:END:

\begin{align*}
\min_{x\in\mathbb{R}^2}\quad & \tfrac12\big((x_1-2)^2 + (x_2-2)^2\big)\\
\text{s.t.}\quad & x_1 + x_2 \le 2\\
& x_1 \le 1\\
& x_2 \le 1
\end{align*}

The unconstrained minimizer $(2,2)$ is cut off. The feasible region lies inside
$\{x_1\le 1\}\cap\{x_2\le 1\}$, so the closest feasible point to $(2,2)$ is the
componentwise maximum $(1,1)$, giving $f^\* = 1$. All *three* inequalities are
active there, so in $\mathbb{R}^2$ the active constraint gradients
$(1,1),(1,0),(0,1)$ are linearly dependent: **LICQ fails**. The multipliers form
a one-parameter family $\lambda = (\lambda_1, 1-\lambda_1, 1-\lambda_1)$,
$\lambda_1\in[0,1]$ — dual degeneracy, exactly the configuration that makes a
naive active-set method cycle or settle on a wrong working set.

Run: =adversary/runs/2026-07-19_qp-active-set_licq-degenerate-vertex.py=
(x0 = (-1,-1), i.e. deep inside the region, so the method must *add* all three
constraints).
