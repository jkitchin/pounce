#+TITLE: Adversary Run: Max-Entropy Gibbs Distribution with a Near-Zero Tail
#+DATE: 2026-07-22

* Problem
:PROPERTIES:
:FAMILY: exp
:CLASS: exponential cone -- ill-conditioning / bad scaling (optimal probability driven to ~1e-12, exp-cone arguments spanning 0 .. -28, stressed to -700)
:SOURCE: Boyd & Vandenberghe, "Convex Optimization" (2004) Sec. 3.5 (max-entropy / exponential family); Cover & Thomas, "Elements of Information Theory" 2e, Thm 12.1.1. Instance constructed so the optimum is closed form.
:KNOWN_OPTIMAL: H* = lam*b + log Z = 0.007301297755369 (lam = 1.4)
:N_VARIABLES: 10 (p_0..p_4, epigraph z_0..z_4)
:N_CONSTRAINTS: 2 equalities + 5 exponential cones (15 conic rows)
:END:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\max_{p\in\mathbb{R}^5}\quad & H(p) = -\sum_i p_i \log p_i\\
\text{s.t.}\quad & \sum_i p_i = 1,\qquad \sum_i a_i p_i = b,\qquad p \ge 0
\end{aligned}
\]

with $a = (0,5,10,15,20)$. The instance is *constructed*: fix $\lambda = 1.4$,
set $Z=\sum_i e^{-\lambda a_i}$, $p^\star_i = e^{-\lambda a_i}/Z$, and
$b = \sum_i a_i p^\star_i = 0.004563571266093$. By the maximum-entropy theorem
the unique maximizer under these moment constraints is exactly this Gibbs
distribution, and

\[ H^\star = \lambda b + \log Z = 0.007301297755369 \]

which was verified against $-\sum p^\star \log p^\star$ to 1e-13 inside the
script.

*Why this is the dangerous regime.* The exp-cone arguments span $0$ to $-28$
and the optimal probabilities span twelve orders of magnitude:

: p* = (9.99088118e-01, 9.11050437e-04, 8.30770463e-07, 7.57564603e-10, 6.90809499e-13)

so $p_4^\star \approx 6.9\times10^{-13}$ sits essentially *on* the boundary of
$\operatorname{dom}(p\log p)$ -- the classic failure mode for an exp-cone IPM.

** Cone encoding (verified)
pounce/Clarabel/MOSEK orientation $K_{\exp}=\{(x,y,z): y e^{x/y}\le z,\ y>0\}$.
Then $z_i \le -p_i\log p_i \iff p_i e^{z_i/p_i} \le 1 \iff (z_i, p_i, 1)\in K_{\exp}$.
Slack $s = h - Gx$ is built per cone as $(z_i, p_i, 1)$; objective
$\min -\sum z_i$. This matches the orientation confirmed in the 2026-07-19
=exp_logreg_l2= run.

Oracle agreement first: ECOS vs CLARABEL relative gap 3.63e-08, so the two
independent oracles agree before pounce is compared to anything.

pounce is *more accurate than ECOS* (3.52e-08 vs 3.75e-08) and about 30x worse
than CLARABEL -- all three are in the same 1e-8 noise band, which is the
expected accuracy floor for an exp-cone HSDE on a problem whose optimum has a
6.9e-13 component. Speedup vs CLARABEL 0.6x, vs ECOS 1.0x (absolute gap
< 2 ms, far below the 0.1 s regression threshold).

Primal quality of the pounce point:

: |sum p - 1|  = 1.54e-09
: |a.p - b|    = 3.38e-12
: min p        = -8.88e-11   (tiny negative tail entry)
: |p - p*|     = (3.6e-08, 7.2e-08, 3.0e-08, 4.3e-09, 8.9e-11)

The slightly negative $p_4 = -8.9\times10^{-11}$ is *not* a pounce defect: both
oracles return tail entries that are equally wrong in absolute terms
(ECOS gives $p_4 = 1.97\times10^{-9}$, CLARABEL $1.86\times10^{-10}$, against a
true $6.9\times10^{-13}$). Every component below ~1e-9 is beneath all three
solvers' absolute tolerance, and the objective is insensitive to it because
$p\log p \to 0$ there.

* Cross-check / Verification
Two independent oracles (cvxpy ECOS and cvxpy CLARABEL, both on
=cp.entr=, DCP-clean) were required to agree with each other (3.63e-08) before
pounce was judged, and both were independently checked against the closed-form
$H^\star$.

*Scaling stress sweep.* The same construction was re-run with
$\lambda \in \{1.4, 3, 6, 12, 24, 35\}$, pushing the exp-cone arguments from
$-28$ down to $-700$ (i.e. $p_{\min}$ from 6.9e-13 to 9.9e-305, the float64
underflow boundary):

pounce returned =optimal= in every case with no NaN/Inf, no crash, and wall
time pinned at 0.003 s throughout. Its worst absolute error over the whole
sweep is 6.7e-07 (at lam = 3), versus 1.2e-07 for ECOS and 2.9e-08 for
CLARABEL. For lam >= 6 the problem degenerates ($b \to 0$, $H^\star \to 0$,
$p^\star \to e_0$) and all three solvers return ~1e-7 absolute noise around
zero, pounce included; ECOS is the most accurate there and pounce the least,
but the spread is one order of magnitude in a regime where the true objective
is below every solver's absolute tolerance. That is a tolerance-floor
observation, not a defect: no oracle-confirmed wrong answer, no wrong status,
no failure to converge.

* Verdict
PASS. On the primary instance -- an exponential-cone entropy problem whose
optimal probability vector spans twelve orders of magnitude and whose smallest
component ($6.9\times10^{-13}$) sits on the domain boundary -- pounce's
=solve_socp= returns =optimal= in 3.4 ms with a relative objective error of
3.5e-08 against a closed-form optimum, beating ECOS and matching CLARABEL to
within the exp-cone accuracy floor. Equality feasibility is 1.5e-09 / 3.4e-12.
Stressing the construction to exp-cone arguments of $-700$ (probabilities at
the float64 underflow boundary) produced no crash, no NaN, no wrong status, and
no error above 6.7e-07 absolute, with the two oracles bracketing pounce rather
than contradicting it. There is nothing here to file: the sub-1e-9 disagreement
in the tail *components* is shared by all three solvers and is a property of
the problem's conditioning, not of pounce's implementation.
