Every prior ~sensitivity~-family probe in the log (near-LICQ dx/db,
barrier-curvature, ~reduced_hessian~, multi-parameter dx/db, economic
dispatch) checked the PRIMAL sensitivity \(dx^*/db\). This probe instead
checks the classical envelope/duality theorem for the OPTIMAL VALUE:
\(d(\mathrm{obj}^*)/db_i = \pm y_i^*\) (sign depending on the Lagrangian
convention), where \(y^*\) is the equality constraint's own optimal
multiplier -- read directly off a *plain* ~solve_qp~ result, independent
of ~QpSensitivity~'s own factorization.

** Sign convention (not a bug)
The raw comparison \(y^*\) vs \(d\mathrm{obj}/db\) initially showed the
right magnitude but opposite sign (\(y^*=(-3.333,-1.0)\) vs FD
\((3.333,1.0)\)) -- pounce's internal Lagrangian convention is
\(L=f(x)-y^T(Ax-b)\) (stationarity \(\nabla f = A^Ty\) with this sign
giving \(d\mathrm{obj}/db=-y\)), rather than the opposite-sign convention
this probe initially assumed. Flipping the sign in the comparison (not in
pounce) resolves it to \(<10^{-10}\) relative error. Documented here, not
filed -- FORMULATION_ERROR in the adversary script's assumed sign, not a
pounce defect (the magnitude match on the first, unflipped run was already
strong evidence of this).
