* Problem
:PROPERTIES:
:FAMILY: exp
:CLASS: exponential-cone log-barrier (analytic center)
:SOURCE: Boyd & Vandenberghe, "Convex Optimization", Section 8.5.3
:KNOWN_OPTIMAL: -2.1972245773362196
:N_VARIABLES: 6 (2 primal x, 4 epigraph t)
:N_CONSTRAINTS: cones=[("exp",3)]*4, no A/b
:SELECTED_BY: log-rotation

Analytic center of the box $x_1 \in [-2,1]$, $x_2 \in [-1,3]$:

\[
\text{maximize} \quad \log(1-x_1) + \log(x_1+2) + \log(3-x_2) + \log(x_2+1)
\]

Separable per coordinate (box = Cartesian product), so each coordinate's
analytic center is the interval midpoint: $x_1^\star = -0.5$, $x_2^\star = 1.0$.

status with equivalent accuracy; that is the result reported here.
