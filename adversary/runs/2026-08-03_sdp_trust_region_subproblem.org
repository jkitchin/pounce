#+TITLE: Adversary Run: Trust-Region Subproblem via SDP Relaxation
#+DATE: 2026-08-03

* Problem
:PROPERTIES:
:FAMILY: sdp
:CLASS: single dense PSD cone (Shor/moment relaxation) of a nonconvex quadratic program
:SOURCE: Moré & Sorensen (1983) secular-equation TRS algorithm; SDP-relaxation tightness is the classical S-lemma result (Boyd & Vandenberghe App. B; Fortin & Wolkowicz 2004)
:KNOWN_OPTIMAL: -2.1245040322 (via Moré-Sorensen secular equation, closed form)
:N_VARIABLES: 5 (v = [x1, x2, X11, X21, X22])
:N_CONSTRAINTS: cones=[("psd",3), ("nonneg",1)]
:SELECTED_BY: log-rotation
:COVERAGE_TARGET: -
:END:

The trust-region subproblem (TRS) with an *indefinite* Hessian $B$ is a
genuinely nonconvex quadratic program:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\text{minimize} \quad & \tfrac12 x^TBx + g^Tx \\
\text{subject to} \quad & \|x\|_2 \le \Delta
\end{aligned}
\qquad B=\mathrm{diag}(1,-2),\quad g=[1,1],\quad \Delta=1
\]

Despite $B$'s indefiniteness, the S-lemma guarantees the Shor/moment SDP
relaxation (lift $x\mapsto(x,X)$ with $X\succeq xx^T$) is *exactly tight*
for a single ball constraint — this probe tests whether pounce's PSD-cone
solver recovers that exact nonconvex optimum via the convex relaxation:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\text{minimize} \quad & \tfrac12\langle B,X\rangle + g^Tx \\
\text{subject to} \quad & \operatorname{tr}(X)\le\Delta^2,\quad
\begin{bmatrix}1 & x^T\\ x & X\end{bmatrix}\succeq 0
\end{aligned}
\]

Known optimal: -2.1245040322 (secular equation root $\lambda^*=3.0322476$,
$x^*=[-0.24800065,-0.96875987]$).

pounce's recovered $x^*=[-0.24802919,-0.96875255]$ matches the closed-form
minimizer to $x_\infty\text{-err}=2.85\times 10^{-5}$. The recovered
moment matrix $X_p$ is nearly rank-1
($\|X_p - x_px_p^T\|_F = 1.17\times10^{-8}$), confirming the relaxation was
solved to the tight (rank-1) face, not merely to a loose SDP bound.

* Cross-check / Verification

- **cvxpy/CLARABEL** solves the byte-for-byte identical SDP (same moment
  matrix, same trace constraint) via an independent conic solver: agrees
  with pounce to 1.66e-10 relative objective error.
- **Moré-Sorensen secular equation**: for diagonal $B$, $x(\lambda) =
  -(B+\lambda I)^{-1}g$ and $\|x(\lambda)\|=\Delta$ is solved by
  `scipy.optimize.brentq` over $\lambda > -\lambda_{\min}(B) = 2$ (the
  standard, non-hard case, since $g$ has nonzero component along both
  eigenvectors of $B$). This is a closed-form numerical procedure
  independent of any SDP/conic solver.
- The recovered moment matrix's minimum eigenvalue is
  $-1.69\times10^{-9}$ (PSD to solver tolerance) and $\operatorname{rank1\_gap}=1.17\times10^{-8}$,
  directly confirming the S-lemma's tightness guarantee held numerically,
  not just that the objective happened to match.

* Verdict
PASS — pounce's PSD-cone solve of the Shor SDP relaxation recovers the
exact global optimum of the underlying *nonconvex* (indefinite-Hessian)
trust-region subproblem, verified against an independent conic solver
(cvxpy/CLARABEL, 1.66e-10) and an independent closed-form root-finding
oracle (Moré-Sorensen secular equation, 6.46e-09), with the relaxation
confirmed numerically tight (rank-1 recovery, PSD certificate). No
discrepancy found.
