* Problem
:PROPERTIES:
:FAMILY: qp-active-set
:CLASS: separable QP, one equality (budget) + box bounds, sparse active set
:SOURCE: P. Brucker (1984), "An O(n log n) algorithm for quadratic knapsack
  problems", Operations Research Letters 3(3):163-166 (continuous quadratic
  knapsack / water-filling KKT structure); Boyd & Vandenberghe Ch.5 for the
  water-filling KKT form.
:KNOWN_OPTIMAL: -32.932003353 (computed via 1-D scipy.optimize.brentq root-find
  on the monotone KKT multiplier equation, N=10)
:N_VARIABLES: 10
:N_CONSTRAINTS: 1 equality (sum x_i = S) + 10 box bounds (2 active at optimum)
:SELECTED_BY: log-rotation
:COVERAGE_TARGET: - (log-rotation only)
:END:

minimize \sum_i 0.5 q_i x_i^2 - c_i x_i s.t. \sum_i x_i = S, l_i \le x_i \le u_i

KKT stationarity gives x_i(\lambda)=clip((c_i+\lambda)/q_i, l_i, u_i); the
equality residual g(\lambda)=\sum x_i(\lambda)-S is monotone in \lambda, so
its root is a 1-D bisection independent of any linear-algebra QP solver.
Only 2 of 10 bounds are active at the optimum (sparse active set), a
different regime from the prior box-only test's denser activity.
