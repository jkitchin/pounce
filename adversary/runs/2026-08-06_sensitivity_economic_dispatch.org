#+TITLE: Adversary Run: Economic-Dispatch QP Sensitivity dP_i/dD
#+DATE: 2026-08-06

* Problem
:PROPERTIES:
:FAMILY: sensitivity
:CLASS: parametric dx/db, applied power-systems QP (equal-incremental-cost)
:SOURCE: Wood, Wollenberg & Sheble, "Power Generation, Operation, and Control", Ch. 3 (economic dispatch / equal incremental cost criterion)
:KNOWN_OPTIMAL: closed form dP_i/dD = (1/(2 a_i)) / sum_j(1/(2 a_j))
:N_VARIABLES: 4 generators
:N_CONSTRAINTS: 1 equality (power balance sum P_i = D), box [Pmin,Pmax] (inactive at this D)
:SELECTED_BY: log-rotation
:COVERAGE_TARGET: -
:END:

Quadratic generator cost \(C_i(P_i) = a_i P_i^2 + b_i P_i\), minimized
subject to power balance \(\sum_i P_i = D\) and box limits (inactive at
\(D=400\) by construction, so the equal-incremental-cost closed form
applies). At an interior optimum \(2a_iP_i+b_i=\lambda\) for all \(i\);
differentiating the KKT system w.r.t. \(D\) gives

\[ \frac{dP_i}{dD} = \frac{1/(2a_i)}{\sum_j 1/(2a_j)} \]

independent of \(D\) and \(b\) while the active set is unchanged. 4
generators, \(a=(0.02,0.03,0.015,0.025)\), \(b=(20,18,22,19)\), \(D_0=400\).

* Cross-check / Verification
- Closed-form equal-incremental-cost formula (independent of any solver,
  derived directly from the KKT stationarity condition): agrees with
  pounce's ~parametric_step~ to 5.55e-17 (machine epsilon).
- Central finite-difference re-solve: NOT done by calling pounce's own
  ~solve_qp~ twice (that would be pounce-vs-pounce); instead the bordered
  KKT system \(\begin{psmallmatrix}2A&\mathbf1\\\mathbf1^\top&0\end{psmallmatrix}\)
  was assembled and solved directly with ~numpy.linalg.solve~ at \(D_0\pm1\),
  fully independent of pounce. Agrees to 1.04e-13.
- ~sens.ill_conditioned~ and ~sens.weakly_active_indices~ checked and
  confirmed clean (no bound/inequality near-activity) before trusting the
  first-order predictor, per the QpSensitivity docstring's own caveat
  about near-LICQ over-damping (issues #284, #328) — not applicable here
  since this problem has generous box slack by construction.

* Verdict
PASS — pounce's parametric sensitivity matches both the closed-form
economic-dispatch derivative and an independently-assembled
finite-difference oracle to machine precision. A genuinely new applied
problem class (power-systems dispatch) for the ~sensitivity~ family,
distinct from the generic KKT dx/db probes already in the log.
