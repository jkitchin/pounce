* Problem
:PROPERTIES:
:FAMILY: lp
:CLASS: network-flow (min-cost flow LP)
:SOURCE: Cormen, Leiserson, Rivest, Stein, "Introduction to Algorithms" 3rd ed., Figure 24.6 (Dijkstra worked example, single-source shortest paths from s)
:KNOWN_OPTIMAL: 9
:N_VARIABLES: 10 (one per directed edge)
:N_CONSTRAINTS: 5 equalities (flow conservation)
:SELECTED_BY: log-rotation
:COVERAGE_TARGET: -
:END:

Vertices \(\{s,t,x,y,z\}\); directed edges/weights: s\(\to\)t:10, s\(\to\)y:5,
t\(\to\)x:1, t\(\to\)y:2, x\(\to\)z:4, y\(\to\)t:3, y\(\to\)x:9, y\(\to\)z:2,
z\(\to\)x:6, z\(\to\)s:7.

\[
\min_f \; c^\top f \quad \text{s.t.} \quad N f = e_s - e_x, \quad f \ge 0
\]

where \(N\) is the node-arc incidence matrix. Because the network-flow
polytope with a unit supply/demand is totally unimodular, the LP optimum is
guaranteed integral and equal to the shortest s\(\to\)x path length.
CLRS's worked example publishes \(d(x)=9\) (path s\(\to\)y\(\to\)t\(\to\)x,
weights 5+3+1).
