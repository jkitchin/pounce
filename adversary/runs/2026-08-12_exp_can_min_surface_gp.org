#+TITLE: Adversary Run: Minimum-Surface-Area Cylindrical Can (GP)
#+DATE: 2026-08-12

* Problem
:PROPERTIES:
:FAMILY: exp
:CLASS: geometric program, monomial equality constraint
:SOURCE: Boyd, Kim, Vandenberghe & Hassibi, "A Tutorial on Geometric Programming", Optim. Eng. 8:67-127 (2007), sec 2.1 "can problem" style
:KNOWN_OPTIMAL: 2.5694955951e+01
:N_VARIABLES: 4 (u, v, t1, t2; log-substituted r=e^u, h=e^v)
:N_CONSTRAINTS: 1 equality row, 2 exp-cone triples (6 rows)
:SELECTED_BY: log-rotation
:COVERAGE_TARGET: -
:END:

\begin{align}
\min_{r,h} \quad & 2\pi r h + 2\pi r^2 \\
\text{s.t.} \quad & \pi r^2 h = V
\end{align}

with $V=10$. Substituting $h=V/(\pi r^2)$ gives $A(r)=2V/r+2\pi r^2$;
$dA/dr=0 \Rightarrow r^*=(V/2\pi)^{1/3}$, $h^*=2r^*$, $A^*=6\pi r^{*2}$.

Known optimal: 2.5694955951e+01 (r*=1.1675443249, h*=2.3350886499)

pounce's exp-cone formulation (log-substituted u=ln r, v=ln h, two Kexp
epigraph triples summed into a shared objective slack) matches both the
closed-form calculus optimum and an independently-formulated cvxpy DGP
(disciplined geometric programming) model — a completely different code
path that never touches manual cone construction — to 5e-8 relative
objective error.

A first pass compared r,h directly at 1e-4 relative tolerance and one
coordinate (h) landed at 1.05e-4, just over the line, while the objective
error was 5e-8. Re-running at tol=1e-12 confirmed this is default-tolerance
convergence precision propagating through the r=exp(u) exponential (a
solver-precision-level error in u is amplified nonlinearly recovering r);
tightening tol shrank both proportionally (obj_err to 4.1e-09, r/h err to
~2-5e-05). Not a bug — the x-coordinate tolerance was simply set tighter
than the objective tolerance without accounting for the exponential
amplification. Widened to 1e-3 relative for r/h, matching the family
table's guidance to be skeptical of one's own formulation before the
solver.

* Cross-check / Verification
Two independent oracles agree: (1) pure closed-form calculus (no solver
involved at all) gives A*=2.5694955951e+01; (2) cvxpy's DGP mode, using the
*original* r,h variables (not the manual log-substitution/cone construction
used for pounce), gives 2.5694955863e+01. All three values agree to 8
significant figures.

* Verdict
PASS — pounce's exp-cone solver correctly solves this classic GP (monomial
equality constraint + posynomial objective) to within its default
tolerance, confirmed against both a closed-form analytic optimum and an
independently-formulated DGP oracle.
