\[
f(x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4) = 100(x_1^2-x_2)^2 + (x_1-1)^2 + (x_3-1)^2 + 90(x_3^2-x_4)^2
  + 10.1[(x_2-1)^2+(x_4-1)^2] + 19.8(x_2-1)(x_4-1)
\]

Global minimum \(f^*=0\) at \(x^*=(1,1,1,1)\) (unique zero of a sum of
squares plus a cross term that also vanishes there). Unlike the
2-variable quartics already logged (Rosenbrock, Styblinski-Tang, Beale,
six-/three-hump camel), this is a genuine 4-variable degree-4 polynomial —
Hilbert's 1888 theorem (nonneg poly in \(\le 2\) vars, degree \(\le 4\) ⟹
SOS) does not apply, so exactness of the degree-2 relaxation was not
assumed going in. The expanded 14-term monomial dict fed to
`sos_minimize` was independently verified against the closed formula at 20
random points (max err 4.55e-13) before use.
