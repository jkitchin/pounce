* Problem

:CLASS: inequality-constrained, unbounded variables, high-degree polynomial
:SOURCE: Hock & Schittkowski, /Test Examples for Nonlinear Programming Codes/, Lecture Notes in Economics and Mathematical Systems 187, Springer 1981, problem 100, p. 121 (orig. Asaadi 1973, problem 2)

\begin{align*}
\min_{x\in\mathbb{R}^7}\;& (x_1-10)^2 + 5(x_2-12)^2 + x_3^4 + 3(x_4-11)^2 \\
                        &\quad + 10x_5^6 + 7x_6^2 + x_7^4 - 4x_6x_7 - 10x_6 - 8x_7 \\
\text{s.t.}\;& g_1: 127 - 2x_1^2 - 3x_2^4 - x_3 - 4x_4^2 - 5x_5 \ge 0 \\
             & g_2: 282 - 7x_1 - 3x_2 - 10x_3^2 - x_4 + x_5 \ge 0 \\
             & g_3: 196 - 23x_1 - x_2^2 - 6x_6^2 + 8x_7 \ge 0 \\
             & g_4: -4x_1^2 - x_2^2 + 3x_1x_2 - 2x_3^2 - 5x_6 + 11x_7 \ge 0
\end{align*}

with $x_0 = (1,2,0,4,0,1,1)$, $f(x_0) = 714$.

Published optimum: $f^* = 680.6300573$ at
$x^* = (2.330499,\,1.951372,\,-0.4775414,\,4.365726,\,-0.6244870,\,1.038131,\,1.594227)$.
Constraints $g_1$ and $g_4$ are active at $x^*$; $g_2,g_3$ are slack by
$2.5\times10^2$ and $1.4\times10^2$.

All five analytic gradients (objective and $g_1..g_4$) were checked against

confirming the transcription of both objective and constraints.

** =pounce verify= on the emitted .nl
The Pyomo model was written to =.nl=, solved with the pounce CLI, and the
resulting =.sol= checked with =pounce verify=:

#+begin_example
max constraint violation: 1.268e-6  at g1
objective at x*: 6.8063005593e2
KKT stationarity residual: 4.565e-13
VERDICT: REJECTED — solution VIOLATES the canonical constraints
#+end_example

This =REJECTED= is **not** a pounce defect. Running the *identical* =.nl=
through the Ipopt binary and verifying *Ipopt's* =.sol= gives a byte-for-byte
comparable outcome — the same 1.268e-6 violation at $g_1$ and the same
=REJECTED= verdict (rc 20). Both solvers converge to the same point; the
verifier's default =--feas-tol 1e-6= is an *absolute* threshold that does not
scale with constraint magnitude, and $g_1$ has RHS 127, so the *relative*
violation is $1\times10^{-8}$. With =--feas-tol 1e-5= (or 1e-4, which is
Ipopt's own default =constr_viol_tol=) pounce's solution verifies cleanly
(rc 0).

Observation worth recording (not a finding, since it is oracle-symmetric):
=pounce verify='s default absolute feasibility tolerance of 1e-6 is stricter
than the acceptance criterion of any solver it is meant to audit, so it will
routinely reject converged solutions of problems whose constraints carry
large constants.

* Verdict
PASS. pounce reproduces the published optimum to 9.0e-11 relative error,
