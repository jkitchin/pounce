\begin{align}
\min\quad & 2x + 3y + z \\
\text{s.t.}\quad & x + y + z = 6 \\
& x - y = 1 \\
& y \ge 0,\ z \ge 0,\ x \ \text{free}
\end{align}

Substituting the equalities collapses the feasible set to a segment
parametrized by \(y \in [0, 2.5]\), with objective \(3y + 7\) — strictly
increasing in \(y\), so the unique minimizer is at \(y=0\): \(x^*=1, y^*=0,
z^*=5\), \(f^*=7\).
