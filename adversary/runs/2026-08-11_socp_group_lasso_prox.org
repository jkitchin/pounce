#+TITLE: Adversary Run: L2-norm (group-lasso) proximal operator as SOCP with P+cone
#+DATE: 2026-08-11

* Problem
:PROPERTIES:
:FAMILY: socp
:CLASS: quadratic objective (P) combined with a plain SOC block -- prior
  socp probes in the log use linear-only (c-only) objectives; this exercises
  the HSDE driver's handling of a nonzero P alongside a SOC constraint.
:SOURCE: Parikh & Boyd, "Proximal Algorithms" (2014) sec 6.5.3
  (block/group soft-thresholding); Boyd, Parikh, Chu, Peleato, Eckstein,
  ADMM monograph (2011) sec 6.2.
:KNOWN_OPTIMAL: closed form, see below (two cases)
:N_VARIABLES: 6 (5 + epigraph var t)
:N_CONSTRAINTS: 1 SOC block, dim 6
:SELECTED_BY: log-rotation
:COVERAGE_TARGET: - (log-rotation driven; coverage-combined.sh skipped per environment adaptations)
:END:

The proximal operator of $\lambda\|\cdot\|_2$ is the well-known block
soft-threshold:
\[
\mathrm{prox}_{\lambda\|\cdot\|_2}(b) = b\cdot\max\!\left(0, 1-\tfrac{\lambda}{\|b\|_2}\right)
= \arg\min_x \ \tfrac12\|x-b\|_2^2 + \lambda\|x\|_2 .
\]
Reformulated (dropping the additive constant $\tfrac12\|b\|_2^2$, which
cancels identically out of every objective comparison below since both
solvers omit it) as an SOCP over $z=(x,t)\in\mathbb R^{n+1}$:
\[
\min_z\ \tfrac12 x^\top x - b^\top x + \lambda t \quad\text{s.t.}\quad (t,x)\in\mathrm{SOC}.
\]
$P=\mathrm{blkdiag}(I_n,0)$, $c=(-b,\lambda)$, and the cone is encoded via
$G=-\Pi$, $h=0$ where $\Pi$ permutes $t$ to the front of $z$, giving
$s=h-Gz=\Pi z=(t,x)$.

Two cases, $n=5$, seed 7, $\|b\|_2 = 2.939587$:
- (a) $\lambda = 0.3\|b\|_2$ -- shrinkage active, $x^*\ne 0$ (interior optimum).
- (b) $\lambda = 1.5\|b\|_2$ -- $x^*=0$, $t^*=0$ exactly: the optimum sits
  at the *apex* of the cone, the degenerate boundary case the family notes
  flag as the encoding trap.

Known optimal (case a): $x^*=(-1.18633679,\ 0.78377262,\ -0.17245415,\ 0.6257025,\ 1.33837063)$,
shifted objective $-2.1170872496\times10^{0}$.
Known optimal (case b): $x^*=0$, shifted objective $0$.

Both cases agree with cvxpy/CLARABEL and with the exact closed-form
proximal-operator formula to $\sim 10^{-9}$. Case (b) is the more
demanding check: pounce returns $x$ on the order of $10^{-10}$ (not
exactly 0, as expected from an interior-point method approaching a
boundary point), which is well inside tolerance and matches cvxpy's own
residual at the same point.

* Cross-check / Verification
Independent oracle: cvxpy/CLARABEL, formulated directly with
=cp.SOC(t, x)= (no shared code with the pounce cone-encoding path).
Independent closed form: the textbook group-lasso/block-soft-threshold
formula, evaluated directly from $b$ and $\lambda$ (no dependence on
either solver). All three agree on both the interior and the boundary
case.

* Verdict
PASS -- pounce's =solve_socp= correctly handles a nonzero quadratic term
$P$ combined with a plain SOC cone, on both an interior optimum and a
degenerate optimum sitting exactly at the cone's apex, matching cvxpy and
the closed-form proximal-operator solution to $\sim 10^{-9}$.
