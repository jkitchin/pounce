#+TITLE: Adversary Run: Minimize Sum x_i^5 on the Simplex (Power Cone)
#+DATE: 2026-08-12

* Problem
:PROPERTIES:
:FAMILY: power
:CLASS: p-norm epigraph (p=5, odd exponent) mixed with nonneg orthant
:SOURCE: power-mean/Lagrange-symmetry argument; Boyd & Vandenberghe, "Convex Optimization" sec 3.1.5
:KNOWN_OPTIMAL: 3.90625000e-03 (= 1/256)
:N_VARIABLES: 8 (x_0..x_3, t_0..t_3)
:N_CONSTRAINTS: 1 equality row; cones = [("nonneg",4)] + [("pow",0.2)]*4
:SELECTED_BY: log-rotation
:COVERAGE_TARGET: -
:END:

\begin{align}
\min_x \quad & \sum_{i=1}^4 x_i^5 \\
\text{s.t.} \quad & \sum_i x_i = 1, \quad x \ge 0
\end{align}

By Lagrange stationarity ($5x_i^4=\lambda$ for all active $i$) and convexity
of the objective over the simplex, $x^*=(1/4,1/4,1/4,1/4)$ is the unique
global minimizer.

This is the first power-family probe to mix an explicit ``("nonneg", d)``
cone block with power-cone triples in the *same* ``G``/``h`` partition, and
the first to use an odd exponent (p=5) where nonnegativity has to be
supplied separately from the cone itself (the pow cone bounds \|x\|, not x;
without the nonneg block the problem is unbounded below). pounce handled
the mixed-cone partition correctly.
