#+TITLE: Adversary Run: Markowitz Portfolio with a Hard Risk (SOC) Budget
#+DATE: 2026-07-19

* Problem
:PROPERTIES:
:FAMILY: socp
:CLASS: SOC constraint active/tight at the optimum, coupled with a linear equality
:SOURCE: MOSEK Modeling Cookbook v3.3, Sec. 3.3.3 "Markowitz portfolio optimization"; Boyd & Vandenberghe, /Convex Optimization/, Sec. 4.4.1 and Ex. 4.52 (maximize return subject to a risk bound)
:KNOWN_OPTIMAL: -0.20918477976 (min form); max return 0.20918477976 -- exact closed form
:N_VARIABLES: 3
:N_CONSTRAINTS: 1 equality + one SOC of dimension 4
:END:

\begin{align*}
\text{maximize} \quad & \mu^\top x \\
\text{subject to} \quad & \mathbf{1}^\top x = 1 \\
& \| \Sigma^{1/2} x \|_2 \le s
\end{align*}

with
\[
\Sigma = \begin{bmatrix} 0.10 & 0.02 & 0.01 \\ 0.02 & 0.08 & 0.03 \\ 0.01 & 0.03 & 0.12 \end{bmatrix},\quad
\mu = (0.10,\ 0.20,\ 0.15),\quad s = 0.30 .
\]
pounce minimizes, so the run solves \(\min -\mu^\top x\) with the same constraints.

** Closed-form optimum (no solver involved)
With \(L = \operatorname{chol}(\Sigma)\) and \(y = L^\top x\) the problem becomes
\(\max \tilde\mu^\top y\) s.t. \(a^\top y = 1,\ \|y\|_2 \le s\), where
\(\tilde\mu = L^{-1}\mu\), \(a = L^{-1}\mathbf 1\). Splitting
\(y = a/\|a\|^2 + y_\perp\) with \(y_\perp \perp a\) gives
\(\|y\|^2 = 1/\|a\|^2 + \|y_\perp\|^2\), hence
\[
\text{OPT} = \frac{\tilde\mu^\top a}{\|a\|^2} + r\,\Big\|\Big(I - \tfrac{aa^\top}{\|a\|^2}\Big)\tilde\mu\Big\|,\qquad
r = \sqrt{s^2 - 1/\|a\|^2},
\]
and \(x^\star = L^{-\top}\big(a/\|a\|^2 + r\,P_\perp\tilde\mu/\|P_\perp\tilde\mu\|\big)\).
Note \(1/\|a\|^2 = 1/(\mathbf 1^\top\Sigma^{-1}\mathbf 1) = 0.0458889\) is the
classic minimum-variance portfolio variance (std 0.2142169), so \(s = 0.30\)
leaves \(r > 0\): strictly feasible, and the SOC constraint is *tight* at the
optimum. Exact values:
\(x^\star = (-0.15571656,\ 1.02797903,\ 0.12773753)\), OPT (max) \(= 0.20918477976\).

This is the deliberate contrast with all five prior =socp= runs (least squares,
Chebyshev minimax, robust LS, min enclosing ball, Fermat-Weber): there the cone
was an epigraph of the objective, here it is a hard *active* constraint on the
cone boundary interacting with an equality.

pounce matches the analytic optimum to 7.2e-10 relative and the analytic
minimizer to 1.6e-08 in the infinity norm. Feasibility at the returned point:
\(\sum x = 0.999999994\) and \(\|L^\top x\| = 0.300000003\) -- i.e. the SOC is
tight to 3e-9, confirming the constraint is genuinely active and pounce lands on
the cone boundary rather than in the interior. pounce is ~2.4x faster than
CLARABEL, though the absolute gap (2 ms) is far below the 0.1 s performance
threshold, so no performance claim either way.

* Cross-check / Verification
- Cone encoding re-derived from the documented convention \(s = h - Gx \in K\):
  for =("soc", 4)= the block is \((s_0, s_{1:3})\) with \(s_0 \ge \|s_{1:3}\|\).
  Set \(h_0 = s\), \(G_{0,:} = 0\) so \(s_0 = 0.30\); and \(G_{1:,:} = -L^\top\),
  \(h_{1:} = 0\) so \(s_{1:3} = L^\top x\), giving \(\|L^\top x\| \le 0.30\),
  i.e. \(\|\Sigma^{1/2}x\| \le s\) since \(\Sigma = LL^\top\). The equality
  \(\mathbf 1^\top x = 1\) is passed via =(A, b)=, not as a cone row.
- The closed form is self-checked in the script by three assertions before any
  solver runs: \(\mathbf 1^\top x^\star = 1\), \(\|L^\top x^\star\| = s\)
  (to 1e-12), and \(-\mu^\top x^\star = \) KNOWN_OPTIMAL.
- Three independent cvxpy solvers (CLARABEL, SCS, ECOS) on a DCP model built
  directly from the mathematical statement (=cp.norm(L.T @ x, 2) <= s=), all
  agreeing with pounce and with the closed form. SCS's 3.4e-06 deviation is its
  own first-order accuracy, not a pounce discrepancy -- CLARABEL and ECOS both
  agree with pounce to ~1e-9.

* Verdict
PASS -- pounce reproduces the exact closed-form optimum of a Markowitz SOCP with
a binding risk budget to 7.2e-10 relative error in the objective and 1.6e-08 in
the minimizer, while satisfying the equality to 6e-9 and sitting on the SOC
boundary to 3e-9. Two high-accuracy interior-point oracles (CLARABEL, ECOS)
independently confirm. No finding.
