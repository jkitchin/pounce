Minimize $\sum_i w_i \|x-p_i\|_2$ over points
$p=\{(0,0),(4,0),(2,3),(-1,2)\}$, weights $w=\{1,2,1,3\}$. No closed form
for a non-symmetric weighted instance; cross-checked against (a) cvxpy/
Clarabel (independent conic solve, DCP via ~cp.norm~) and (b) Weiszfeld's
algorithm (1937 fixed-point iteration, converged to $\sim10^{-14}$ step
size) -- two mutually-independent oracles, neither of which trusts pounce
or each other's cone encoding.
