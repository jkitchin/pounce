* Problem
  min  -x*y*z
  s.t. x*y + y*z + z*x = 3

  Classical isoperimetric-type result: for fixed surface area, the cube
  maximizes enclosed volume. Nonconvex (bilinear terms in both objective and
  constraint) — exercises the filter-IPM's restoration/line-search machinery,
  not just a convex fast path.

* Formulation skepticism

    constraint gradient degenerates) without constraining the true optimum,
    which sits comfortably in the interior at (1,1,1).
  - Verified the unique fully-symmetric positive critical point is a genuine
    local (in fact global, by AM-GM) max of volume for this class of
    problem — not a saddle — via the sign of the bordered Hessian
    informally (all three oracles below independently converge to the same
    point from different starts, which is itself evidence against a saddle).
